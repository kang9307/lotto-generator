👨‍🎓 1. Youth Support Programs

🌟 Cheongnyeon Doyak (Youth Leap) Savings Account 2025

Open to all young adults aged 19-34!

Up to 50,000,000 KRW + Government Matching Contributions

📌 Cheongnyeon Doyak Account Details

  • Eligibility: Young adults aged 19-34 (no income restrictions)
  • Benefits: When you save up to 700,000 KRW per month, the government adds matching contributions
  • Savings Period: 5 years (government contributions are clawed back upon early withdrawal)
  • How to Apply: Visit any bank branch nationwide or apply online
  • Application Deadline: December 31, 2025

🎯 Other Youth Support Programs

  • Cheongnyeon Wolse (Youth Rent) Special Support: 200,000 KRW/month x 12 months (ages 19-34)
  • K-Digital Training: Full tuition coverage + training stipend
  • Youth Startup Grant: Up to 100,000,000 KRW + mentoring
  • Cheongnyeon Naeil Chaeum Gongje (Youth Tomorrow Savings): Up to 18,000,000 KRW accumulated over 3 years

👷‍♂️ 2. Worker Support Programs

🏖️ Worker Vacation Subsidy Program

Up to 400,000 KRW in domestic travel support!

Pay 200,000 KRW out of pocket → Enjoy 400,000 KRW worth of travel

📌 Worker Vacation Subsidy Program Application Guide

  • Eligibility: Employees of small and medium-sized enterprises and small business owners
  • Subsidy Amount: Worker 200,000 KRW + Employer 100,000 KRW + Government 100,000 KRW
  • Slots Available: First-come, first-served for 150,000 applicants in 2025
  • How to Apply: Vacation Shop online mall (www.휴가샵.kr)
  • Validity Period: Through December 31, 2025

💼 Other Worker Support Programs

  • Worker Living Stability Fund: Up to 20,000,000 KRW at a low 2% annual interest rate
  • Worker Children's Tuition Aid: 70% coverage of college tuition
  • Worker Health Checkup: Full comprehensive health screening covered
  • Vocational Skills Development: 80% coverage of training costs for employed workers

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 4. Family Support Benefits

👶 Childcare Support Programs

  • Adong Sudang (Child Allowance): 100,000 KRW/month (all children under age 8)
  • Yangyuk Sudang (Childcare Allowance): 200,000 KRW/month (when not using daycare)
  • Cheotmannam Iyong-gwon (First Meeting Voucher): 2,000,000 KRW per newborn
  • Bumo Geupyeo (Parental Benefit): 700,000 KRW/month (up to 12 months after birth)

🏠 Housing Support Programs

  • Youth Monthly Rent Support: 200,000 KRW/month x 12 months
  • Newlywed Jeonse (key-money deposit) Loan: Up to 350,000,000 KRW (1.2% annual interest)
  • Multi-Child Family Jeonse Loan: Up to 400,000,000 KRW (1.0% annual interest)
  • Jugeo Geupyeo (Housing Benefit): Average 160,000 KRW/month (bottom 47% income bracket)

👴 Senior Support Programs

  • Gicho Yeon-geum (Basic Pension): Up to 330,000 KRW/month (age 65 and older)
  • Senior Employment Support: Up to 710,000 KRW/month
  • Chimae Ansim Center (Dementia Care Center): Dementia screening and treatment support
  • Senior Health Checkup: Free comprehensive health screening

🏢 5. Local Government Special Programs

🗺️ Major Local Government Subsidies

  • Seoul: Cheongnyeon Sudang (Youth Allowance) of 500,000 KRW/month x 6 months
  • Gyeonggi Province: Cheongnyeon Gibon Sodeuk (Youth Basic Income) of 250,000 KRW/month x 12 months
  • Busan: Cheongnyeon Digital Baedang (Youth Digital Dividend) of 1,000,000 KRW/year
  • Daegu: Youth Startup Grant of up to 50,000,000 KRW

🎯 How to Check Local Government Subsidies

Visit your local Jumin Center (community service center) or your local government's website to check. Each region has unique support programs, so be sure to look carefully!

📝 6. Application Tips and Precautions

⚠️ Don't Miss These Programs with Approaching Deadlines

Some programs close on a first-come, first-served basis!

  • Worker Vacation Subsidy: First-come, first-served for 150,000 slots
  • Cheongnyeon Doyak Account: Deadline December 31, 2025
  • Youth Monthly Rent Special Support: Closes when budget is exhausted

📋 Pre-Application Checklist

  • ID Card: Jumin Deungnokjeung (resident registration card), driver's license, or passport
  • Income Verification: Earned income withholding receipt, business registration certificate, etc.
  • Bank Account Copy: Account for subsidy deposit
  • Employment Certificate: Required for worker-targeted subsidies
  • Jumin Deungrok Deungbon (Resident Registration Abstract): For verifying family relations

⚠️ Important Precautions When Applying

  • No Duplicate Applications: You cannot apply for the same subsidy more than once
  • Penalties for False Applications: Providing false information may result in legal penalties
  • Strict Deadlines: Applications cannot be submitted after the deadline
  • Document Preparation: Incomplete documents will result in application rejection

💡 Tips to Improve Your Application Success Rate

  • Prepare in Advance: Gather all required documents ahead of time
  • Accurate Information: Enter accurate details when filling out the application
  • Apply Early: For first-come, first-served programs, quick applications are crucial
  • Use Inquiry Services: Contact the responsible agency with any questions
