1. Government Support Programs

Worker Vacation Support Program 2025 (Geunroja Hyuga Jiwon Saeop)

Up to 400,000 KRW in domestic travel support!

You pay 200,000 KRW -> Get 400,000 KRW in total travel funds

Worker Vacation Support Program Details

  • Eligibility: Employees of small and medium enterprises (SMEs), small business owners, and non-profit organizations
  • Funding: 400,000 KRW per person (200,000 KRW from the employee + 100,000 KRW from the employer + 100,000 KRW from the government)
  • Application period: First-come, first-served starting January 24, 2025 -- limited to 150,000 people
  • Validity: Must be used by December 31, 2025
  • Where to use: Purchase domestic travel products at the Hyuga Shop (Vacation Shop) online mall

Pro Tip on Support Programs

If your company is not participating in the Worker Vacation Support Program, try asking your HR department about it. Many companies simply do not know it exists, and employee requests often lead to participation!

Key Transportation Savings Points

  • Book early: Booking 2-3 weeks in advance can save up to 50%
  • Use memberships: Korail and airline memberships offer additional discounts
  • Off-peak timing: Early morning or late-night departures are 20-30% cheaper
  • Bundle deals: Transportation + accommodation packages save an extra 10-20%

3. How to Save on Accommodation

Savings by Accommodation Type

  • Hotels: Booking through aggregator sites averages 25% less than direct booking
  • Pensions (Korean vacation rentals): Direct booking + cash payment gets you 10-15% off
  • Guesthouses: Use Airbnb or Yeogiotte (Korean booking app) coupons
  • Camping: Regular camping saves 70% compared to glamping

Accommodation Savings Hack

Timing is everything: The first week of July and the last week of August are 30-40% cheaper than peak season. Also, Sunday-through-Thursday stays are about 40% less than weekends!

4. Food Cost Savings Tips

Saving by Meal Type

  • Breakfast: Simple cooking at your accommodation (using convenience store ingredients)
  • Lunch: Explore local restaurants (eat at places outside the tourist zone)
  • Dinner: Traditional market street food + takeout
  • Snacks: Buy local specialties directly from producers

5. Cutting Activity Costs

How to Save on Activities

  • Free experiences: Take advantage of programs run by local governments
  • Bundle deals: Buy combo packages for multiple activities
  • Group discounts: Groups of 4+ get about 20% off when booking together
  • On-site payment: Paying at the venue instead of online can sometimes save 10%

Activity Selection Tip

Nature first: Plan around free natural attractions like beaches, hiking trails, and parks first, then pick just 1-2 paid activities. The best memories do not always come with a price tag!

6. Smart Trip Planning

Budget Planning Principles

  • Set a budget: Allocate 60% of your total budget to essential expenses
  • Compare prices: Check at least 3 sites before booking
  • Maximize discounts: Stack credit card rewards and membership benefits
  • Emergency fund: Set aside 10% of your budget as a contingency
