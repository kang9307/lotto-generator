1. Complete Overview of Notion AI Core Features

1.1 AI Writing Assistance

Notion AI's writing assistance dramatically improves the efficiency of creating work documents. You can summon AI on any page by pressing /ai or the Space key, and it supports a wide range of writing tasks.

  • Auto-summarize: Concisely summarizes lengthy documents, meeting notes, and reports, focusing on key points. Even a planning document that spans dozens of pages can be distilled to its core content in seconds - especially useful for busy executives and managers. You can also specify the summary length and format (bullet points, paragraphs, etc.).
  • Translation: Instantly translates from Korean to English, Japanese, Chinese, and many other languages. This is an essential feature for professionals collaborating with global teams, enabling multilingual document management within Notion without separate translation tools. It particularly excels at translating business terminology thanks to its strong understanding of business context.
  • Tone adjustment: Converts the same content into formal, informal, friendly, or professional tones. When internal reports, blog posts, and customer emails require different tones, you can quickly change the writing style while keeping the content intact.
  • Editing and expanding: Polishes rough drafts into grammatically correct and easy-to-read sentences. You can expand short notes into detailed documents or, conversely, condense lengthy text into concise form.
  • Brainstorming support: Prompts like "Suggest 10 ideas on this topic" help generate creative ideas. This is highly effective for broadening your thinking during the early planning stages.

1.2 AI Q&A (Knowledge Base Search)

Notion AI Q&A is a groundbreaking feature that turns your entire workspace into one massive knowledge base. Instead of simple keyword searches, you can ask questions in natural language, and it synthesizes information scattered across your workspace to deliver accurate answers.

  • Workspace-wide search: For natural language questions like "What was last quarter's marketing budget?" or "What's the onboarding process for new employees?", it searches across all pages, databases, and wikis in your workspace to provide comprehensive answers.
  • Source attribution: When AI provides answers, it displays the source pages alongside them, allowing you to navigate directly to the original document for verification. This plays a crucial role in ensuring information reliability.
  • Connected app search: It also searches information from external services integrated with Notion, such as Slack, Google Drive, and Confluence, and incorporates them into answers. You can access organizational knowledge distributed across multiple tools from a single entry point.
  • Context understanding: Rather than simple keyword matching, it understands the intent and context of your questions. When you ask about "recent job postings," it prioritizes the latest information by date, and when you ask about "marketing team-related" topics, it focuses the search on the marketing team workspace.

1.3 AI Autofill (Database)

By adding AI properties to Notion databases, AI automatically analyzes each entry and fills in values. This feature dramatically reduces repetitive data organization tasks.

  • Auto-classification: In a customer inquiry database, each inquiry is automatically classified into categories like "Technical Support," "Billing Inquiry," or "Feature Request." The task of manually categorizing hundreds of inquiries is now automated.
  • Auto-tagging: Analyzes the content of blog posts or documents and automatically generates relevant tags. Without manually entering keywords, AI identifies the core topics of the content and assigns tags accordingly.
  • Auto-summarize: Automatically generates one-line summaries for each database entry (e.g., meeting notes, project documents). Being able to grasp the content of each item at a glance in list view significantly improves work efficiency.
  • Sentiment analysis: Automatically analyzes positive, negative, or neutral sentiment in customer feedback and review data. This can be used to quickly gauge and respond to customer voices.
  • Custom AI properties: You can write your own prompts and add custom AI analyses as properties. For example, setting a prompt like "Summarize the top 3 risk factors for this project" as an AI property will automatically perform risk analysis for every project entry.

1.4 AI Document Generation

Starting from a blank page is daunting for everyone. Notion AI automatically generates drafts of various document types from simple prompts.

  • Report drafts: Request something like "Draft a Q1 2026 sales report. Include revenue trends, key achievements, and areas for improvement," and it generates a structured report template with draft content for each section.
  • Proposals: Simply input a project overview, and it creates a proposal draft with sections for objectives, scope, timeline, resources, and risk management. You can quickly establish the framework and then fill in the details.
  • Emails and announcements: Requests like "Write an email to all employees about the year-end bonus" enable fast creation of business emails and internal announcements.
  • Pitch decks and company introductions: Enter the key features of your service or product, and it generates drafts of proposals or company introductions for clients. Sales teams can use this to quickly prepare customized proposals for each customer.

2. Real-World Workplace Scenarios

2.1 Project Management

Notion provides project management capabilities that rival dedicated tools like Jira, Asana, and Trello. Without adopting a separate project management tool, you can manage the entire project lifecycle from planning to completion with Notion alone.

  • Kanban board: Visually manage task status with a "To Do - In Progress - Done" kanban board. Change status via drag and drop, and use filters and sorting to display only tasks for specific assignees or priorities. Each kanban card can include detailed descriptions, checklists, file attachments, and comments, keeping all task context in one place.
  • Timeline view: Visualize project schedules with a Gantt chart-style timeline view. Set task dependencies, mark milestones, and get an at-a-glance overview of overall project progress. It also supports automatic adjustment of related tasks when schedules change.
  • Sprint management: Configure sprint boards for teams using agile methodology. Set two-week sprints, manage sprint goals and tasks, and keep sprint review and retrospective documents all in the same space.
  • AI project analysis: Ask Notion AI questions like "Which tasks in the current project are delayed?" or "What are the high-priority tasks that need to be completed this week?" and it analyzes the database to provide immediate answers.

2.2 Meeting Notes Automation

Meetings are part of daily work life, but writing meeting notes is a time-consuming chore. Notion AI enables you to significantly automate the meeting notes process.

  • Real-time meeting notes organization: Even if you only jot down brief notes during a meeting, AI transforms them into structured meeting notes afterward. It neatly organizes discussion points, decisions, and participant opinions by agenda item.
  • Auto-extract action items: AI automatically extracts action items in the format of "who does what by when" from meeting notes. Extracted action items can be automatically linked to the project database for follow-up management.
  • Meeting summary sharing: Summarize the key points of a 30-minute meeting into 3 lines and quickly share with team members who couldn't attend. It generates summaries in a format that can be immediately forwarded via messengers like Slack.
  • Meeting notes template automation: Create templates for recurring meetings (weekly meetings, monthly reviews, etc.), and meeting notes are generated with the same structure each time for consistency. AI can also reference previous meeting notes to automatically suggest the agenda for the next meeting.

2.3 Knowledge Management (Team Wiki)

Organizational knowledge is the most valuable asset, but without systematic management, it quickly becomes scattered and lost. Notion is an ideal platform for building team wikis, and combined with AI search, it becomes even more powerful.

  • Internal wiki construction: Build an internal wiki in Notion that systematically organizes company policies, work processes, technical documents, and FAQs. Using hierarchical structures (nested pages) and databases, you can logically organize vast amounts of information.
  • Instant answers via AI search: For questions like "How do new employees apply for leave?" or "What's the process for overseas business trip expense reimbursement?", AI finds relevant information in the wiki and provides instant answers. Since AI handles repetitive questions that typically flow to HR or admin teams, the workload on those staff is significantly reduced.
  • Onboarding automation: Create an onboarding guide as a wiki and let new employees resolve their questions instantly through AI Q&A. New hires can find and learn the information they need on their own without mentors having to explain everything individually.
  • Keeping knowledge up to date: Add a "Last reviewed date" property to wiki pages, and AI automatically identifies outdated documents and recommends updates. This enables you to create a living wiki that always maintains the latest information.

2.4 Personal Productivity

Notion excels not only as a team tool but also as a personal productivity maximizer. You can manage both work and personal life from a single Notion workspace.

  • To-Do management: Build anything from simple checklists to advanced task management with priorities, deadlines, and project-based categorization. Ask AI every morning "What are my 3 most important tasks today?" and it recommends tasks based on deadlines and priorities.
  • Daily journal: Manage a daily journal as a database, recording your daily work log, lessons learned, and things you're grateful for. AI automatically generates weekly/monthly reviews so you can identify your growth patterns.
  • Goal tracking: Set quarterly and annual goals and track progress. Implement the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) framework as a Notion database to systematically manage objectives and key results.
  • Study notes: Record and structure what you learn from online courses, books, and seminars. AI summarizes your learning content and tags it with relevant keywords for easy retrieval later.

3. Mastering Notion Databases

3.1 Database Basics

Notion's database is not just a simple table. It's a multi-faceted system that can display a single database in multiple views. Being able to look at the same data from different perspectives depending on the purpose means a single database can fulfill diverse work requirements.

  • Table view: Similar to Excel with rows and columns, optimized for quickly entering and browsing large volumes of data. Filter, sort, and group functions let you extract exactly the data you need.
  • Board view: Kanban board format that visually categorizes items by status. Ideal for project management, CRM pipeline management, and more.
  • Calendar view: Displays items on a calendar based on date properties. Used for content publishing schedules, schedule management, and deadline tracking.
  • Gallery view: Displays items as cards for visually managing entries that include images. Useful for portfolios, product catalogs, and design asset management.
  • Timeline view: Arranges items along a time axis in Gantt chart format. Ideal for project schedule management and roadmap visualization.

3.2 Relational Databases (Relation & Rollup)

Notion's relational database feature allows you to connect different databases to create complex information structures. This is the key feature that elevates Notion from a simple document tool to a full-fledged business tool.

  • Relation: Establishes connections between two databases. For example, linking a "Projects" database with a "Tasks" database lets you immediately see the tasks belonging to each project. Connecting a "Customers" database with a "Contracts" database lets you view each customer's contract history at a glance.
  • Rollup: Aggregates values from connected databases. You can calculate the average completion rate of all tasks linked to a project, or sum the total value of all contracts linked to a customer. This enables data-driven decision-making without a separate spreadsheet.
  • Real-world example: To build a CRM system for a sales team, connect four databases - "Companies," "Contacts," "Deals," and "Activity Log" - through relations. From a single company page, you can view the contact list, active deals, and recent activity history for that company, creating a comprehensive CRM.

3.3 Essential Formula Patterns

Notion's Formula feature enables automatic calculations and conditional displays within databases. With the major Formula 2.0 update in 2024, capabilities were significantly enhanced.

  • Date calculations: The formula dateBetween(prop("Deadline"), now(), "days") automatically calculates the number of days remaining until the deadline. Various date operations are possible, including D-Day countdowns, elapsed day calculations, and week number calculations.
  • Conditional display: Conditional statements like if(prop("Status") == "Done", "OK", "In Progress") display different values based on status. You can nest complex conditions to implement business logic.
  • Progress calculation: Divide the number of completed tasks aggregated via Rollup by total tasks using a formula to automatically calculate project progress. Display the percentage with round(prop("Completed Tasks") / prop("Total Tasks") * 100) and visualize it with a progress bar.
  • Text concatenation: Combine values from multiple properties to generate new text. For example, combine a project code, date, and assignee to automatically generate a unique task ID.

3.4 Leveraging AI Properties

AI properties are special properties where AI automatically analyzes each database entry and generates values. They enable semantic analysis that was previously impossible with traditional formulas or Rollups.

  • Custom AI analysis: Set a prompt like "Based on the content of this item, determine the priority as High/Medium/Low," and AI reads and analyzes the content of each item to automatically assign priorities.
  • Keyword extraction: Automatically extracts key keywords from lengthy document content. This makes search and classification easier without manual tagging.
  • Next action suggestions: Analyzes the current state of a project or task and automatically suggests the next action to take. For instance, when the status is "Customer requested a quote," it might suggest "Send the quote and make a follow-up call in 3 days."

4. Top 5 Recommended Templates

Notion's power is maximized through templates. A single well-crafted template can save hours of setup time. Here are the 5 most useful templates for professionals.

  1. Project Tracker: A template that integrates task management, milestones, and resource allocation by project. Table, board, and timeline views are pre-configured, making it ready to use regardless of team size or project type. AI properties automatically analyze the risk level and estimated duration of each task.
  2. OKR Management: A template for systematically setting and tracking quarterly Objectives and Key Results. Company, department, and individual OKRs are connected through relations, and progress is automatically aggregated via Rollup. It includes quarterly review pages, enabling a seamless retrospective process.
  3. Reading Notes: A template for recording key takeaways, memorable passages, and action items from books you've read. Filter by genre or rating, and AI auto-generates one-line reviews by summarizing the book's content. Manage book covers visually in gallery view.
  4. Habit Tracker: A template for checking daily habits and tracking consecutive achievement days. Formulas automatically calculate completion rates, and calendar view provides a monthly achievement overview at a glance. Track up to 10 habits simultaneously, including exercise, reading, meditation, and learning.
  5. CRM (Customer Management): A small-scale CRM template for managing sales pipelines and customer relationships. It consists of 4 relational databases: Companies, Contacts, Deals, and Activity Log. View deal status by stage in board view, and automatically sum projected revenue via Rollup.
Template Tips

External Integrations

Notion's true power is unleashed through integrations with other tools. Let's look at the key external integration features.

  • Slack integration: Sharing a Notion page in a Slack channel displays a preview, and you can create Notion pages or add database entries directly from Slack. You can even use Notion AI Q&A directly in Slack to instantly search for needed information during conversations.
  • Google Calendar integration: Sync Google Calendar events to a Notion database, managing schedules and tasks in a single space. Adding an event in Notion is automatically reflected in Google Calendar as well.
  • GitHub integration: Development teams can link GitHub issues, PRs (Pull Requests), and commits to Notion project boards to track development progress. Code review status, deployment schedules, and more can be managed centrally in Notion.
  • Figma integration: Embed Figma design files in real-time on Notion pages, managing design mockups and planning documents in one place. This dramatically improves collaboration efficiency between designers and planners.

Automation

Automating repetitive tasks saves time and reduces errors. Here are the automation methods available in Notion.

  • Built-in Notion automations: Automatically execute actions when specific conditions are met in a database. For example, set rules to automatically record the completion date when a task status changes to "Done," or automatically notify the assignee when a new entry is added.
  • Notion API: The official API enables you to build custom automations that directly integrate external systems with Notion. Programmatically manipulate Notion databases using Python, JavaScript, and other languages to automate data synchronization with existing business systems.
  • Zapier / Make integration: No-code automation tools like Zapier and Make (formerly Integromat) allow you to connect hundreds of apps with Notion without any coding. Scenarios include automatically registering entries in a Notion database when an email is received, or automatically saving customer information to Notion when a form is submitted.
  • Button blocks: Notion's button blocks let you perform complex operations with a single click. For example, pressing a "Generate Weekly Report" button creates a report template with the current week's dates automatically filled in.
