💡 About the Mindfulness Timer

A web-based mindfulness tool for mental rest and focus. It combines a customizable timer with natural sounds to provide an optimal environment for meditation and concentration.

🎯 Key Features

  • Customizable timer: Set the duration in minutes
  • Circular progress ring: See remaining time at a glance
  • Four nature sounds: Rain, forest, ocean waves, and campfire
  • Sound mixer: Adjust each volume to craft your own ambient blend
  • Real-time alerts: Remaining time shown in the browser tab title
  • Completion alert: A gentle chime plays when the timer ends

🌱 How to Use It

  • Meditation and mindfulness: Step away from daily stress and clear your mind
  • Focused work: Create a deep-focus environment for studying or working
  • Short breaks: Recharge mentally during a quick pause
  • Sleep aid: Calm your mind before bed
  • Yoga and stretching: Use as background audio to enhance focus during exercise

🎵 Sound Descriptions

  • 🌧️ Rain: A calm, steady rhythm that soothes the mind
  • 🌳 Forest: Birdsong and rustling wind for natural tranquility
  • 🌊 Ocean Waves: A regular wave rhythm for deep relaxation
  • 🔥 Campfire: Warm, cozy crackling for a feeling of safety and comfort

❓ How to Use

  1. Set the time
    • Enter the desired duration in minutes in the "Timer Settings" section
    • Anywhere from 1 to 999 minutes is supported
    • 5-30 minutes is recommended for meditation, 25-50 minutes for focused work
  2. Mix the sounds
    • Pick the sounds you want from the soundscape mixer
    • Adjust each volume slider to your preferred level
    • Play multiple sounds together to build your perfect ambient environment
    • Setting a slider to 0 automatically stops that sound
  3. Start the timer
    • Click the "Start" button to begin
    • The circular progress ring visualizes time elapsed
    • You can also see the remaining time in the browser tab title
  4. Control the timer
    • Pause: Use this to take a short break
    • Reset: Returns the timer to its initial duration
    • An alert sound plays automatically when the timer ends
  5. Tips for effective use
    • Use it in a quiet, comfortable environment
    • Headphones or earbuds are recommended
    • Use it regularly to build a healthy habit
    • Experiment to find the sound combination that suits you best
⚠️ Notes
  • Closing the browser will stop the timer
  • The timer may pause if your computer enters sleep mode
  • Allow audio permission in your browser to play sounds
  • Sound playback may stutter on an unstable internet connection
