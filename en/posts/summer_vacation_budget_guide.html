Government Support Programs

Worker Vacation Support Program 2026

400,000 won in domestic travel points under the standard split

Employee 200,000 + employer 100,000 + government 100,000 = 400,000 won

2026 notice summary (checked 2026-09-29)

  • Eligibility: Employees of mid-sized companies, SMEs, small businesses, non-profits and social welfare corporations or facilities
  • Split: Standard: government 100,000, employer 100,000, employee 200,000 won. Mid-sized firms in their fifth or later cumulative year: government 50,000, employer 150,000, employee 200,000 won
  • Application: Company-level online, first-come, first-served from 14:00 on January 30, 2026. Sources give the recruitment size as either 100,000 or about 145,000 people, so confirm on the program website
  • Validity: From the time points are credited until December 31, 2026
  • Where to use: Domestic travel products at the Vacation Shop online mall (lodging, transport, activities, admission tickets)

Check participation and open status first

The company, not the individual, applies, so tell the relevant department about the program and ask whether it participates. It is first-come, first-served and closes when the budget is used up, so check the program website (vacation.visitkorea.or.kr) to see if applications are still open in the second half of 2026. The 2025 conditions in the original ("from January 24, 150,000 people") were last year's and have been removed.

Key transport-saving points

  • Book early: Cheaper seats often sell out first as departure nears, so compare as soon as dates are set
  • Use memberships: Carrier memberships and partner cards come with conditions and limits, so read the terms
  • Adjust timing: Avoiding peak hours widens your options, but the real gap differs by route
  • Bundles: Compare transport-plus-lodging packages side by side with the separate-booking total

Why there are no percentages

The 2025 version carried discount rates such as "up to 50%" with no traceable source, so they were removed. Rates differ by route, season and seat, so compare the total shown on the booking screen.

How to Save on Accommodation

Saving strategies by lodging type

  • Hotel: Compare booking-platform and direct totals on the same terms, including cancellation rules
  • Pension (cottage): Ask about direct-booking or cash terms, and confirm the refund policy in writing
  • Guesthouse / short-term rental: Judge by the total including coupon conditions, cleaning fees and extra-guest fees
  • Camping: Ordinary camping tends to be cheaper than glamping, but include gear purchase or rental costs

Lodging tip

Avoiding peak weeks and weekends tends to lower rates, but the gap differs by region and season. The 2025 figures of "30-40%" and "40%" had no traceable source and were removed, so compare weekday and weekend rates for your own dates.

Food Cost Savings Tips

Saving by meal type

  • Breakfast: Simple cooking at the lodging (convenience store or grocery ingredients)
  • Lunch: Restaurants away from the main tourist strip
  • Dinner: Market food and takeout
  • Snacks: Buy local specialties directly from groceries or markets

Cutting Activity Costs

Ways to cut activity costs

  • Free experiences: Check programs run by local governments and public institutions
  • Package deals: Compare bundled activity products with the separate-purchase total
  • Group discounts: Discounts with headcount conditions depend on each facility, so ask when booking
  • Payment method: Compare price and refund terms for on-site versus advance payment

Activity selection tip

Pick free nature experiences such as beaches, trails and parks first, and narrow paid activities to the one or two you really want. The 2025 figures of "20%" and "10%" had no traceable source and were removed.

Smart Trip Planning

Trip-planning saving principles

  • Set a budget: Secure essentials such as lodging and transport first, then allocate the rest (any ratio is a personal planning example, not a standard)
  • Compare: Compare totals across sellers on the same terms, including fees and cancellation rules
  • Card and membership perks: Check discount conditions, monthly caps and excluded categories in the issuer's disclosures and terms
  • Overseas payments: For exchange, check the fee built into the rate and the preferential-rate terms; for cards, the international brand and issuer fees. Paying in Korean won at an overseas merchant (DCC) adds fees, so paying in local currency is advised, and issuers offer a DCC block service

Keep a reserve

Setting aside part of the budget for cancellations, delays and unexpected costs is safer. The 2025 claim of "50% average savings if you use every tip" had no traceable source and was removed. For overseas trips, also check travel insurance separately.

Wrapping Up: Check and Compare

A summer vacation budget shrinks when you confirm the program terms and compare transport, lodging and food on the same totals. The principles here set out that order and do not guarantee any amount of savings.

Amounts and ratios in this article are general information and planning examples, not financial advice or a product recommendation. Exchange rates and airfare and lodging prices change constantly and this article makes no forecast of them. Check card benefits, exchange and overseas payment fees, and program terms in the latest disclosures and terms of the card issuer, bank or operator, and consult a financial professional if needed.

# Summer Vacation Budget Checklist - Program, Comparison, Overseas Payments

As of: 2026-09-29
General information based on domestic Korean travel and Korean cards and banks. It contains no exchange-rate or price forecasts and recommends no specific company, and it is not financial advice.

---

## 1. Check the program

- [ ] Does your company take part in the Worker Vacation Support Program? (company-level application, first-come, first-served, ends when the budget runs out)
- [ ] Does your company type qualify? (mid-sized, SME, small business, non-profit, social welfare corporation or facility)
- [ ] What is your share? (standard employee 200,000 won; also 200,000 for mid-sized firms in year five or later)
- [ ] Can you use the points before the 2026-12-31 deadline?
- [ ] Did you check the current application status on the program website yourself?

---

## 2. Compare before booking

- [ ] Did you compare transport, lodging and activities on totals with the same terms (cancellation rules, fees)?
- [ ] Did you check the conditions, monthly caps and exclusions of card and membership discounts in the terms?
- [ ] Did you compare packages side by side with the separate-booking total?
- [ ] Did you save the refund and cancellation rules?

---

## 3. If traveling overseas

- [ ] Did you check the fee built into the rate and the preferential-rate terms for each exchange method?
- [ ] Did you check the international brand and issuer fees of your overseas card in the issuer's disclosures?
- [ ] Have you decided to pay in local currency, not won (DCC), at the payment screen?
- [ ] Have you decided whether to apply for the issuer's DCC block service?
- [ ] Did you check travel insurance separately?
