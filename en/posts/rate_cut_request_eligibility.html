Why Korea's Rate Cut Request Right Is So Often Declined

A right to ask, not a right to receive - separating the grounds from the refusals, structurally (as of August 2026, Korean system)

Aug 26, 2026 Finance & Economy
This is general information about a Korean legal mechanism, not financial advice, and it does not recommend any product or institution. Rate levels and reduction sizes differ by institution, product and individual creditworthiness, so no figures are given here. Whether a request is accepted, and on what terms, is settled by the financial institution's own review and is not determined by anything written here. The description reflects the position as of August 2026; for a specific case, ask the institution holding the contract or the Financial Supervisory Service consumer line on 1332.

A right to ask, not a right to receive

You were promoted, your income went up, so you filed the request expecting your loan rate to follow, and a few days later a short text message tells you the request was declined. This is the single most common experience with the mechanism, and it almost always begins with one sentence being missed. What the law grants is the asking, not the reduction.

The Korean name says it outright: it is a rate cut request right. Standing to file, standing to have that filing reviewed, and standing to be told the outcome along with its reason - that is the full content of the right. The outcome itself is not inside it. Which is why a refused request is usually not a sign that the system malfunctioned but a sign that it ran exactly as designed.

The distinction matters because it decides where you set expectations. Treat it as a right to a reduction and a refusal becomes an injustice with someone to blame. Treat it as a right to ask and a refusal becomes information - a reading of which branch you were blocked on, and of what would have to change before an outcome could differ. The rest of this piece is about separating those branches.

Where the right is written down

For bank lending the root is Article 30-2 of the Banking Act. It provides that a party to a credit agreement with a bank may request a rate reduction on the ground of improved credit standing, and the detail is filled in as you go down the hierarchy.

  • Banking Act, Article 30-2 - the enabling provision that a credit agreement counterparty may request a reduction on the ground of improved credit standing
  • Enforcement Decree of the Banking Act, Article 18-4 - the scope of qualifying grounds, the notice deadline and the permitted methods of notice
  • Regulation on Supervision of Banking Business, Article 25-4 - the operating standards a bank must follow
  • Sectors outside banking carry equivalent provisions of their own: Insurance Business Act Article 110-3, Specialized Credit Finance Business Act Article 50-13 and Mutual Savings Banks Act Article 14-2

One point is easy to skip here. These provisions did not only hand consumers a right; they also imposed a duty to inform on the lender. A bank must tell a borrower that a rate reduction may be requested when the credit agreement is concluded, and failing to do so exposes it to an administrative fine of up to 20 million won. That is why the clause is always somewhere in the paperwork.

The fact that different sectors sit under different statutes has practical weight. A bank loan and a card company or savings bank loan share the name of the mechanism but answer to different laws and different supervisory rules. The procedural description below is stated for banks; for other sectors the governing reference is the guidance of the institution holding the contract.

When a request can be filed - the types of grounds

When something has happened that shows credit standing has improved. Korea's Easy Law portal splits the grounds between individuals and non-individuals. Grouping them by which axis moved is more useful than memorising individual events.

AxisConcrete circumstanceWhat review actually looks at
Job and income Employment, promotion, moving jobs, obtaining a professional qualification, higher income Not only the size of income but its stability and durability
Assets and debt Increase in assets, improved net position through debt reduction Whether repayment capacity genuinely grew
Credit assessment A rise in the personal credit score Not just the external score but movement in the institution's internal grade
Transaction record A repayment history carried without arrears, a changed banking relationship Whether the item is one the approval criteria actually use
Businesses and sole proprietors Improved financial condition, a rise in credit rating or credit score Whether the improvement shows in the financial statements

The right-hand column is the point of this section. A request does not stand because the label of your circumstance appears on a list; it turns on whether that circumstance actually moved the assessment the institution uses. A promotion with an unchanged pay structure, so that the documented income figure barely differs, can read as no change during review even though the label qualifies.

  • An external credit score and an institution's internal grade are different numbers. A score that rose in an app has not necessarily lifted the internal grade with it
  • In its 2023 improvement plan the Financial Services Commission required banks to explain, as fully as feasible, the criteria actually used in approval. Items outside the score, such as deposit relationships and arrears history, sit here
  • The same improvement carries different weight depending on when it dates from. It has to be a change measured against the position at the time the contract was signed

Why it gets declined, part one: loans whose rate never came from credit

This first branch is the largest and the one that feels most unfair. These loans are built so that the rate does not move however much creditworthiness improves. Proving improved credit standing changes nothing, not because the mechanism is stingy but because the borrower's credit was never what set that rate.

The categories of loans on which a reduction cannot be requested, as summarised by Korea's government policy briefing service, show that character plainly.

  • Loans under an agreement with an outside body - the rate is fixed in advance by the agreement and its policy purpose, not adjusted by an individual's credit
  • Loans secured on deposits or instalment savings - the collateral is the deposit, so the rate tracks that deposit's yield. Credit has no place to enter
  • Loans with no rate differential by credit grade - the product itself was designed without tiering
  • Loans carrying separate approval conditions - an individual approval condition holds the rate in place, outside the ordinary review path
  • Loans to borrowers restricted under an institution's internal rules - the request never reaches the review track

The problem shows up most often on collateral-led products. The stronger the security, the smaller the share of the rate that borrower credit accounts for, and the less room credit improvement has to push. If there is only one thing to check before filing, it is this: does my creditworthiness enter this loan's rate as a variable at all? If the answer is no, the conclusion is the same however well the evidence is assembled. The product disclosure and terms, or the guidance of the institution holding the contract, will say.

Why it gets declined, part two: already priced in, or short of the threshold

The second branch covers eligible products where there is no room to move. In its February 2023 improvement plan the Financial Services Commission required banks issuing a refusal to break the reason into the categories below. Those five double as a map of refusals.

Reason as notifiedWhat it means structurallyCould time change it?
Not an eligible product The rate on this product is not set by creditworthiness Not while the same contract stands
Already on the lowest rate The floor has already been reached; better credit has nowhere left to go Hard, absent a change to the product structure
No change in the internal credit grade The improving circumstance existed but the assessment did not move Possible once the change is picked up in assessment
Internal grade has not risen enough to permit a reduction It did move, but not far enough to clear the threshold Worth revisiting as more improvement accumulates
Still calculates above the statutory ceiling after the improvement The computed figure sits above the cap and is being held down by it Only a substantial improvement would change the result

The third and fourth categories are what already priced in really looks like. The better the terms you started with, the more often you are stopped here, because a higher starting point puts the next threshold further away for the same size of improvement. It is a paradox worth stating plainly: the stronger your credit, the smaller the remaining room.

  • Acceptance rates move independently of the mechanism's popularity. In its February 2023 material the Financial Services Commission reported that filings were rising while the acceptance rate had fallen from 48.6% in 2019 to 28.8% in the first half of 2022. Reading that as more filings bringing more ineligible filings with them is the natural interpretation
  • The same material changed how the acceptance rate is computed, excluding duplicate filings. Repeat requests from the same borrower had been inflating the denominator
  • Current figures by sector and product are published in the rate cut request comparison disclosure of the Korea Federation of Banks consumer portal. Alongside filings, acceptances, acceptance rate and interest relieved, it also discloses the non-face-to-face filing share and the average reduction, split between household and corporate and between unsecured and secured

Documentation decides the ruling

The same circumstance carries different weight depending on the form the paperwork takes. What a reviewer looks at is not the statement that you were promoted but a document that fixes the fact in settled form. The policy briefing lists the application form, a certificate of employment, a withholding tax receipt for earned income, and supporting evidence of the credit improvement.

  • Documents issued by a third party are stronger. Employment and payroll papers issued by the employer, and income papers that passed through the tax office, outweigh anything you produced yourself
  • Figures for a settled period beat projected ones. A withholding receipt covering a period already paid out is harder to dispute than a contract promising a raise
  • File a comparable pair. You are asserting an improvement, so review is shorter when before and after are visible together
  • Debt reduction is only complete when evidence that the contract was closed accompanies proof that the balance was paid
  • When a request for supplementary documents arrives, the clock on the reply deadline stops. Filing complete the first time turns out to be the fastest route

Documentation does not manufacture an outcome. What it does is get an improvement that already happened fully onto the review table. If there was no improvement, no amount of paperwork changes the result; if there was one, paperwork should never be the reason it goes unseen.

Filing routes and reply - what 10 business days means

The procedure is set out in the rules. In order:

  1. Filing - in branch or remotely. Remote filing runs through a rate cut request menu under the lending section of the institution's website or app
  2. Evidence - the application form goes in together with evidence of the credit improvement. If it falls short, a supplementary request follows
  3. Review - whether the product is eligible, whether the internal assessment moved, and whether the movement clears the threshold
  4. Notice - a bank must give its decision and the reason within 10 business days of receiving the request, by telephone, in writing, by text message, e-mail, fax or a similar method

The 10 business days come with a proviso. The period from the day supplementary documents are requested until the day they are submitted does not count toward it. Sitting on a supplementary request for a few days pushes the reply back by the same margin, and that is the first thing to check when a deadline feels overdue. Because the count is in business days, weekends and public holidays fall out of it as well.

The outcome lands in one of two states, accepted or refused. No third, conditional state exists in the mechanism. So the thing to read in the notice is not the one-line result but the reason recorded beside it. That is where the next move is decided.

After a refusal - read the reason, then plan

If a refusal arrives, this order tends to work best.

  1. Pin down which of the five categories the reason falls into. If the wording is vague, you can ask the institution to be specific. This is where the 2023 plan's requirement that borrowers be able to receive the information used in assessing their credit, alongside the itemised reasons, earns its keep
  2. Separate a structural refusal from one of degree. Not eligible, or already at the floor, means refiling is unlikely to change anything. No movement in grade, or movement that fell short, is a different situation once more improvement has accumulated
  3. Choose when to file again. A uniform rule on how often or how soon a request may be refiled is outside what this piece sets out. Institutions may run their own standards, so the institution holding the contract is the accurate source
  4. If the process itself failed, use the channels. No reply at all, a result delivered with no reason, or never having been told of the right at signing - the institution's complaint desk and the Financial Supervisory Service on 1332 are where those are contested

One distinction is worth holding. Disliking the outcome of a review and a procedure that was not followed are different problems. The first sits in the institution's judgement and is not the kind of thing a complaint reverses; the second is a breach of a duty set by the rules and is worth pursuing. Keeping the notice and the filing record makes that line easy to draw.

Does filing count against you?

This is the most common reason people postpone filing. Start from the structure. A rate cut request ends in one of two outcomes, and the Banking Act provisions on rate cut requests contain no clause that worsens the rate or limit on an existing contract because a request was declined. A refusal leaves the existing contract as it stands and simply does not grant the request.

  • That a credit inquiry takes place for the review is procedurally natural. Assessing whether credit standing improved requires looking at the present standing
  • Whether the filing itself is recorded by a personal credit bureau, and how any record is used in scoring, is not documented here from a public source. It is collected in the section below
  • If the worry weighs heavily, asking the institution holding the contract how filing records are treated is the surest route. Practice can differ by product and by company

How this sits beside refinancing and prepayment

This mechanism is not the only route to a lighter interest burden. The three options turn on different axes, so weighing them on one scale distorts the comparison.

OptionWhat changesThe deciding axis
Rate cut request The contract stays; only the rate terms are reconsidered Whether your credit standing genuinely moved since signing
Refinancing The existing contract is closed and replaced by a new one The gap between new terms and the cost of moving. Underwriting starts over
Repayment or prepayment Principal shrinks, so the base that interest accrues on shrinks The alternative use of spare cash and the prepayment fee terms

If an order is worth suggesting, the rate cut request is usually the first axis to try. It moves no contract, so no fresh underwriting or transaction cost attaches, and whether accepted or refused it leaves a stated reason that feeds the next decision. Where the refusal comes back as not an eligible product, refinancing becomes the remaining axis. Terms and cost arithmetic for switching are set out in the guide to refinancing a Korean mortgage, and the place this right occupies inside a revolving credit line is in what decides a Korean overdraft credit line. If moving the internal grade comes first, raising a Korean credit score is the earlier step.

What is not established here

The points this piece could not source are collected in one place. Blanks were left blank rather than filled with inference.

  • Whether a filing enters credit scoring - no public rule document establishing whether the act of requesting a reduction is recorded by a personal credit bureau, or how any such record is used, has been carried into this article
  • Limits on refiling - no uniform statutory restriction on the number or spacing of repeat requests is asserted here. Whether individual institutions run their own standards, and what those say, is a matter for the institution holding the contract
  • A dedicated appeal route against a refusal - whether the mechanism provides a purpose-built procedure for having a review decision reconsidered is left open. That is a different question from the channels for contesting a procedural breach, namely the institution's complaint desk and the Financial Supervisory Service on 1332
  • Procedural detail outside banking - the enabling provisions for insurance, specialized credit finance and mutual savings banks are cited, but how far their notice deadlines and operating standards match banking is a question for each sector's supervisory rules

A checklist before filing

Working through these in order cuts down wasted attempts.

  1. Does my creditworthiness enter this loan's rate as a variable? If not, the remaining steps are moot. Read the product disclosure and terms first
  2. What has changed since signing? Pin it to a single axis - job and income, assets and debt, credit assessment, or transaction record
  3. Is that change settled in numbers? Pick material from a period already paid out or already closed, not one still promised
  4. Is it evidenced by third-party documents? Bundle employment, income and debt-closure evidence so the before and after read together
  5. Have you counted the reply window? Ten business days from filing, with any supplementary-document period excluded, noted alongside
  6. Are you keeping the notice? A reply carrying a stated reason serves as evidence both for refiling and for contesting the process

Boiled down, the mechanism is one sentence. The asking is your right; the reduction is the review's conclusion. The way to narrow the gap is not to file more often but to bring a real, documented improvement onto an eligible loan. And when a refusal comes back, the single line of reason beside it decides most of what happens next.

This is general information about a Korean legal mechanism, not financial advice, and it does not recommend any product or institution.
