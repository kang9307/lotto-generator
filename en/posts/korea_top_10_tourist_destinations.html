Top 10 Tourist Destinations in South Korea: The Ultimate Travel Guide

From Jeju Island to Gyeongju -- a complete guide to South Korea's finest travel destinations!

📅 June 18, 2025 Life & Practical

🇰🇷 Everything You Need to Know About Korean Tourism

South Korea is a land where 5,000 years of history meets modern charm. We have carefully selected 10 iconic tourist destinations that showcase the country's unique blend of tradition and innovation. This guide covers each region's special character, must-see attractions, and practical travel tips.

📊 Selection Criteria

  • Historical Value: Cultural heritage and historical significance
  • Natural Scenery: Beautiful natural environments and landscapes
  • Accessibility: Transportation options and tourism infrastructure
  • Activities: Variety of tourism activities and experiences
  • Reputation: Domestic and international visitor satisfaction
1

Jeju Island 🏝️

Korea's premier resort destination and a UNESCO World Natural Heritage Site, Jeju Island boasts stunning natural scenery in every season. Its unique volcanic landscape -- including Hallasan Mountain, Seongsan Ilchulbong (Sunrise Peak), and Manjanggul Lava Tube -- harmonizes beautifully with emerald-blue waters.

Scenery:★★★★★5.0/5.0
Access:★★★★★5.0/5.0
Activities:★★★★★4.8/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Hallasan National Park: South Korea's tallest peak (1,947m) and Baengnokdam crater lake
  • Seongsan Ilchulbong (Sunrise Peak): World Natural Heritage site, famous sunrise spot
  • Manjanggul Lava Tube: One of the world's longest lava tubes
  • Hyeopjae Beach: Emerald waters and white sand
  • Jungmun Tourism Complex: Cluster of resorts and theme parks

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: April-June, September-November (mild weather and fewer tourists)

Recommended Duration: 3 nights / 4 days (east-west-south route)

Transportation: Rental car recommended; public transit is also well-developed

2

Gyeongju 🏛️

Often called the "Museum Without Walls", Gyeongju was the capital of the ancient Silla Kingdom for a thousand years and is home to countless cultural treasures. Historical sites of extraordinary value -- including Bulguksa Temple, Seokguram Grotto, Cheomseongdae Observatory, and the Daereungwon Royal Tomb complex -- are scattered throughout the city.

History:★★★★★5.0/5.0
Access:★★★★☆4.2/5.0
Educational:★★★★★4.9/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Bulguksa Temple: UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site, masterpiece of Silla Buddhist art
  • Seokguram Grotto: The pinnacle of Eastern sculptural art
  • Cheomseongdae: The world's oldest surviving astronomical observatory
  • Daereungwon (Royal Tomb Complex): A park of ancient burial mounds of Silla royalty
  • Anapji Pond (Donggung Palace): A Silla royal palace pond, stunning at night

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: Spring (April-May), Fall (October-November)

Recommended Duration: 2 nights / 3 days (city historic sites + Bulguksa/Seokguram)

Local Specialties: Gyeongju-ppang (red bean bread), Hwangnam-ppang, Silla Confections

3

Busan 🌊

Korea's second-largest city, Busan is a stunning port city where the sea meets the mountains. Alongside Haeundae and Gwangalli beaches, diverse attractions like Gamcheon Culture Village and Gukje Market (International Market) make it a multifaceted destination.

Coastal:★★★★★4.9/5.0
Food:★★★★★4.8/5.0
Access:★★★★★4.7/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Haeundae Beach: Korea's most iconic beach, home to the Haeundae Blueline Park
  • Gwangalli Beach: Famous for the nighttime view of Gwangan Bridge
  • Gamcheon Culture Village: Known as "Korea's Machu Picchu," a colorful hillside village
  • Taejongdae: Spectacular cliffs and ocean views
  • Gukje Market & Jagalchi Fish Market: Traditional markets and the seafood capital of Korea

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: May-October (ideal for beach activities and outdoor fun)

Recommended Duration: 2 nights / 3 days (beaches - markets - culture village route)

Local Specialties: Ssiat-hotteok (seed-filled pancake), Busan fish cake, Milmyeon (wheat noodles)

4

Seoul 🏙️

South Korea's capital, Seoul, is a captivating city where tradition and modernity coexist. Joseon Dynasty cultural heritage sites like Gyeongbokgung Palace and Changdeokgung Palace blend seamlessly with the contemporary shopping culture of Myeongdong and Gangnam.

Heritage:★★★★★4.8/5.0
Shopping:★★★★★5.0/5.0
Transit:★★★★★5.0/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Gyeongbokgung Palace: Main Joseon Dynasty palace, featuring the Royal Guard Changing Ceremony
  • Changdeokgung Palace: UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site, including the Secret Garden (Huwon)
  • Bukchon Hanok Village: A neighborhood of traditional Korean hanok houses
  • Myeongdong: The epicenter of shopping and K-Beauty
  • Hangang (Han River) Parks: Public riverside parks, Han River cruises

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: Year-round (each season has its own charm)

Recommended Duration: 3 nights / 4 days (palaces - shopping - Han River - traditional villages)

Transportation: Subway and bus networks are extremely convenient

5

Gangneung ☀️

A premier East Coast destination, Gangneung is known for its beautiful beaches, vibrant coffee culture, and rich historical heritage. Famous for Gyeongpo Beach and Anmok Beach, it also served as a host city for the 2018 PyeongChang Winter Olympics.

Beaches:★★★★★4.7/5.0
Coffee:★★★★★4.9/5.0
Access:★★★★☆4.3/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Gyeongpo Beach: Wide sandy beach framed by beautiful pine forests
  • Anmok Beach: Famous for its "Coffee Street" lined with cafes
  • Ojukheon: Birthplace of the legendary Joseon-era artist Shin Saimdang and her son, scholar Yulgok Yi I
  • Jeongdongjin: Iconic sunrise spot, home to the Hourglass Park
  • Gangneung Olympic Park: Legacy of the 2018 PyeongChang Winter Olympics

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: June-September (summer swimming), December-February (winter ocean views)

Recommended Duration: 1 night / 2 days (beaches - coffee street - historic sites)

Local Specialties: Gangneung coffee, Chodang sundubu (soft tofu), Gamja-tteok (potato rice cake)

6

Jeonju 🏮

The birthplace of Korean taste and style, Jeonju is the ancestral home of the Joseon Dynasty and a center of traditional Korean culture. Famous for its Hanok Village and the iconic Jeonju bibimbap (a mixed rice dish), it is a beautiful city where tradition and modernity blend in harmony.

Culture:★★★★★4.9/5.0
Food:★★★★★5.0/5.0
Access:★★★★☆4.4/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Jeonju Hanok Village: Over 700 traditional hanok houses preserved in a historic district
  • Gyeonggijeon Shrine: Home to the royal portrait of King Taejo, founder of the Joseon Dynasty
  • Omokdae Pavilion: A hilltop viewpoint offering panoramic views of downtown Jeonju
  • Pungnammun Gate: The south gate of the old Jeonju Fortress
  • Jeondong Catholic Cathedral: One of Korea's earliest Western-style churches

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: April-June, September-November (perfect for strolling the Hanok Village)

Recommended Duration: 1 night / 2 days (Hanok Village - traditional market - historic sites)

Local Specialties: Jeonju bibimbap, traditional Korean sweets (hangwa), makgeolli (rice wine)

7

Seoraksan 🏔️

Often called the "Alps of Korea", Seoraksan is the country's top mountain tourism destination. Its majestic peaks, stunning natural scenery across all four seasons, and historic temples such as Sinheungsa and Baekdamsa create a breathtaking experience.

Scenery:★★★★★5.0/5.0
Hiking:★★★★★4.9/5.0
Seasonal:★★★★★4.8/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Daecheongbong Peak: Seoraksan's highest point at 1,708m
  • Ulsanbawi Rock: The iconic massive rock formation of Seoraksan
  • Sinheungsa Temple: An ancient temple home to the Great Unification Buddha statue
  • Biseondae: A legendary valley said to have been visited by celestial maidens
  • Gwongeumseong Fortress: A scenic viewpoint accessible by cable car

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: October (fall foliage), December-February (snowy scenery)

Recommended Duration: 2 nights / 3 days (hiking - hot springs - sightseeing)

What to Bring: Hiking boots, warm clothing, hiking gear

8

Naejangsan 🍁

Korea's most spectacular autumn foliage destination, Naejangsan transforms into a breathtaking tapestry of red, orange, and gold every fall. Centered around Naejangsa Temple, its beautiful valleys and rocky peaks offer a different kind of beauty in every season.

Foliage:★★★★★5.0/5.0
Difficulty:★★★☆☆3.5/5.0
Photos:★★★★★4.9/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Naejangsa Temple: A thousand-year-old temple founded in 636 AD
  • Uhwajeong Pavilion: A pavilion surrounded by stunning fall colors
  • Sinseonmun (Fairy Gate): A natural rock arch formation
  • Geumseon Valley: Crystal-clear water and unique rock formations
  • Seoreabong Peak: Naejangsan's highest viewpoint

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: Late October - early November (peak autumn foliage)

Recommended Duration: Day trip or 1 night / 2 days

Important Note: Extremely crowded during peak foliage season

9

Yeosu 🌉

A harbor city celebrated for its stunning nighttime sea views, Yeosu hosted the 2012 World Expo and is a premier coastal tourism destination. It is famed for the romance of "Yeosu Night Sea" (a beloved Korean song reference), Odongdo Island, and the beautiful night views of Dolsan Bridge.

Nightscape:★★★★★5.0/5.0
Coastal:★★★★★4.8/5.0
Food:★★★★★4.7/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Yeosu Night Sea: Romantic nighttime views along Dolsan Bridge
  • Odongdo Island: Beautiful camellia flowers and bamboo forests
  • Yeosu World Expo Site: Aqua Planet aquarium and Sky Tower
  • Jinnamgwan Hall: A Joseon-era naval headquarters building
  • Hyangiram Hermitage: A seaside temple famous for its sunrise views

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: April-June, September-November (mild climate)

Recommended Duration: 2 nights / 3 days (downtown - Odongdo - Expo site)

Local Specialties: Gat-kimchi (mustard leaf kimchi), Dolsan gat (mustard greens), Seodhoe (raw flatfish)

10

Tongyeong ⛵

The jewel of the Hallyeosudo Waterway, Tongyeong is a coastal city rich with the legacy of Admiral Yi Sun-sin (Korea's legendary naval hero) and the beauty of the southern archipelago. The panoramic view of the Hallyeosudo from the cable car and the fresh local seafood are unforgettable.

Islands:★★★★★4.9/5.0
History:★★★★☆4.5/5.0
Seafood:★★★★★4.8/5.0

Top Attractions

  • Tongyeong Cable Car: Sweeping aerial views of the Hallyeosudo Waterway
  • Yi Sun-sin Park: A park honoring the achievements of the legendary Admiral Yi Sun-sin
  • Dongpirang Mural Village: A hillside village famous for its colorful murals
  • Seopirang Park: A park with panoramic views of downtown Tongyeong
  • Tongyeong Jungang Market: Fresh seafood and local specialties

🎯 Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit: April-June, September-October (pleasant coastal weather)

Recommended Duration: 1 night / 2 days (downtown - cable car - mural village)

Local Specialties: Tongyeong oysters, dried anchovies, Kkul-ppang (honey bread)
