How to Use the Date & Time Calculator

Date Difference

Calculate the duration between two dates.

  1. Select a start date and an end date.
  2. Click the 'Calculate' button.
  3. The number of days, weeks, months, and years between the two dates is displayed.

Add / Subtract Days

Add or subtract a fixed duration from a specific date.

  1. Select a base date.
  2. Choose whether to add or subtract.
  3. Specify the duration to add or subtract using a number and a unit (days, weeks, months, years).
  4. Click 'Calculate' to see the resulting date.

Business Days

Count the number of business days, excluding weekends.

  1. Select a start date and an end date.
  2. Choose whether to exclude weekends (default: Yes).
  3. Click 'Calculate' to see the number of business days and weekend days.

Time Conversion

Convert between seconds, minutes, hours, and days.

  1. Enter the time value you want to convert.
  2. Select the source and target units.
  3. Click 'Convert' to see the converted result.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Does the date difference calculation include both the start and end dates?

A: Yes, both dates are included. For example, from January 1 to January 3 is counted as 3 days.

Q: Can public holidays be excluded from the business day calculation?

A: In the current version only Saturdays and Sundays can be excluded. Support for excluding public holidays is planned for a future update.

Q: How is month-based calculation handled?

A: Month-based calculation uses the actual difference in months between the two dates. For example, January 15 to February 15 is counted as exactly 1 month. When day counts differ (e.g., January 31 to February 28) the result is adjusted appropriately.

Q: How are leap years handled?

A: Leap years are automatically accounted for in every calculation. In leap years that include February 29, that date is correctly reflected in the result.
