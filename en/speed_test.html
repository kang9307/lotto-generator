📋 Tips to Improve Your Speed

  • 📶 Test in an area with a strong Wi-Fi signal
  • 🔒 Stop other devices from using the internet during the test
  • 🔄 Try restarting your router
  • 🕒 Test during off-peak hours when usage is low
  • 📱 Wired connections are typically faster than wireless
  • 🗂️ Close background apps that may be using bandwidth
