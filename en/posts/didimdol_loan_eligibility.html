The Didimdol Loan - Eligibility in Plain Terms

Four gates, which one stops you, how much comes out, and where it parts ways with Bogeumjari - as of August 2026

2026-08-09 Finance & Economy

Read this first - scope, date and limits of this article

This article reflects information as of August 2026 and does not substitute for a loan decision or an eligibility ruling. The Didimdol Loan is a housing policy programme available only to residents of Korea, and its income requirement, net-asset threshold, price ceiling, loan limits, rates and preferential items are adjusted frequently in line with policy direction and fund conditions. The net-asset line in particular changes every year. Whether a specific person qualifies, and how much is actually released, cannot be settled here. Those outcomes rest with the handling bank and the asset review body. Before applying, check the current guidance at the Housing and Urban Fund (nhuf.molit.go.kr) and the MyHome portal (myhome.go.kr) directly. Nothing here recommends taking a loan; it only lays out the material you need to judge for yourself.

What changes because the money comes from a public fund

The full name is the Home Purchase Didimdol Loan. Banks do not lend their own money here. The source is the Housing and Urban Fund, and handling banks such as Kookmin, Nonghyup, Shinhan, Woori, Hana, Busan and iM Bank act as the counter. That structure produces three consequences.

First, the requirements are dense. Income is not the only test; net assets are examined too. A mortgage with a separate asset review attached is unusual in the commercial market. Second, the rate is low. As of August 2026 Didimdol is posted in a band of 2.85 to 4.15 percent, while the Bogeumjari Loan, also a policy mortgage, is published at 4.90 to 5.30 percent. Over a thirty-year term a gap of roughly two percentage points is not a rounding error. Third, obligations continue after the money lands. A residency duty and a one-home duty follow the borrower.

Put simply, the Didimdol Loan is cheap, narrow, and conditional past the closing date. Approaching it on rate alone, without knowing that character, means running into walls mid-screening or after the loan is already running.

If the mechanics of mortgages themselves are the unclear part, start with the complete mortgage loan guide. If you want subscription accounts, special supply and acquisition-tax relief bundled together, the first-time homebuyer guide is the right door. This article stays on one thing: whether you qualify for Didimdol.

Income, assets, the home, ownership - which of the four gates stops you

The scattered requirements collapse into four blocks. Walking them in order settles most cases quickly.

  1. Income gate - is combined household income below the line for your household type
  2. Asset gate - are household net assets 511 million won or less
  3. Home gate - is the collateral 85 square metres or less in exclusive area and valued at 500 million won or less
  4. Ownership gate - does every member of the household, including the head, own no home

In practice the two that stop people most often are gate two and gate three. Most applicants already know their income, and ownership resolves quickly once names on the household register are checked. Net assets, though, are something almost nobody has ever calculated, and in the capital region finding an apartment under 500 million won is itself the binding constraint.

Clearing all four gates means you may fall within scope. It is not approval. Whether the loan is written, and for how much, is decided in the handling bank's screening.

How far the income line moves with household type

The income test is not a single number. As of August 2026 the fund publishes three tiers by household type. General applicants are capped at 60 million won of combined annual income, first-time homebuyers and households with two or more children at 70 million won, and newlywed households at 85 million won.

  • The test is combined. A spouse with no income does not remove the couple basis; unmarried applicants are assessed on their own income alone
  • Income means pre-tax figures as they appear on documentation. Judging by take-home pay leaves people short
  • Dual-income households often trip in a year with a large bonus. The test is the annual total, so monthly salary alone is a misleading basis
  • Self-employed and freelance applicants are assessed on declared income. A wide gap between real earnings and filings shifts both eligibility and limit
  • The newlywed line runs from the marriage registration date, not the wedding date or when the couple moved in together
  • First-time status widens the income line and also connects to a higher LTV. Whether you count as first-time is a separate determination
  • Income brackets are not only an eligibility test; they feed straight into the interest rate. Clearing the line is not the end, because which bracket you land in drives what you pay

Sitting just either side of a line is the awkward case. Guessing here means finding out after the purchase contract that you were never in scope. Stating your income type and documentation format to the Korea Housing and Urban Guarantee Corporation on 1566-9009, or to a handling bank, settles it properly.

Net assets of 511 million won - clearing income and failing here

This is the condition first-time researchers miss most often. The 2026 threshold is net assets of 511 million won or less. Net assets means total assets minus debt, measured at household level.

  • The figure is not fixed. It is revised annually against national survey results. Carrying last year's number over produces a wrong answer
  • The jeonse deposit on your current home counts as an asset. Moving from a capital-region jeonse into a purchase is exactly where this bites
  • Vehicles, financial assets, real estate and other holdings are all counted. A car enters at a residual-value concept and can register larger than expected
  • Debt is deducted, but not every debt counts. The recognised scope is defined, so taking on a credit loan to depress net assets does not work
  • The asset review is a separate procedure. It does not end at the bank counter; a review body checks it independently. The helpline is 1551-3119
  • Comfortably clearing income and then failing on assets is a real combination. Low income with substantial assets puts you out of scope by design

The existence of a net-asset test is the single largest thing separating Didimdol from Bogeumjari, which has no such condition. Anyone near the line is better off listing their asset items and doing the arithmetic before the deposit is paid.

85 square metres and 500 million won - both lines at once

A qualifying borrower with a non-qualifying home gets nowhere. And the home test carries two conditions that must hold simultaneously: price and area. Meeting one is worth nothing.

  • Exclusive floor area of 85 square metres or less. Eup and myeon areas outside the capital region are allowed 100 square metres
  • Appraised value of 500 million won or less. Newlywed households and those with two or more children are allowed up to 600 million won
  • The target is a registered dwelling: apartments, row houses, multi-family units and detached houses
  • So-called geunsaeng villas registered as neighbourhood facilities, and officetels not classified as housing, cannot serve as collateral
  • The measure is exclusive area, not the supply area used in sales brochures. The unit Koreans call a 34-pyeong sits in the 84 square metre range and passes; the next size up mostly does not
  • Price means appraised value, which is not necessarily the number written on the contract

A common misreading is that a 490 million won purchase price passes automatically. The judgement runs on appraised value, so skipping this check before contract means the problem surfaces after the deposit is committed. In the other direction, an appraisal below the contract price also lowers the base for the LTV calculation and shrinks the limit.

The area condition is the hardest to undo. Income and assets can shift with timing, but a home above 85 square metres will not qualify for Didimdol under any combination. Drawing that line at the shortlisting stage saves the most trouble.

How much comes out - the limit is the smallest of four numbers

This is where expectations break. The ceiling and the amount actually released are different things. The final figure is the smallest of the following, each calculated separately.

  1. Product ceiling - the maximum set for your household type
  2. LTV figure - appraised value multiplied by the LTV ratio
  3. DTI figure - the amount whose annual principal and interest stays inside the ratio against income
  4. Amount actually needed - purchase price minus your own funds. You cannot borrow beyond it

Numbers make it concrete. On a 400 million won apartment at 70 percent LTV, the LTV figure is 280 million won. The general ceiling of 200 million won is smaller, so that becomes the cap at this stage. Then DTI is run, and if annual repayments exceed 60 percent against income the figure is cut further. The reasoning that a 500 million won home yields 320 million won does not hold.

The item most often forgotten in DTI is existing debt. Credit loans, car instalments and student loan repayments all enter the calculation. Taking on a large credit loan shortly before applying shrinks the mortgage limit by the same logic, and clearing a nearly-repaid loan in advance can improve it. Note, though, that the net-asset calculation recognises debt only within a defined scope, so adding debt to pass the asset test does not work.

The 80 percent first-time LTV comes with a fixed application route. It has to go through the fund's online channel or a handling bank, and homes in the capital region or regulated zones do not receive the uplift at all.

Reading the grid, the thing that matters most is the bracket boundary. Combined income of 40.5 million and 39.5 million won feel identical in daily life, but they land one row apart, which is 0.35 percentage points at a thirty-year term. Bonus timing alone can move the bracket, so anyone near an edge has reason to confirm the documentation basis for their application date first.

  • Newlywed and first-time reductions apply for up to five years from the release date, not across a full thirty-year term
  • The subscription savings reduction is tiered by how long the account has been held. Longer accounts earn more

  • Even with everything applied, a final rate below 1.5 percent is raised back to 1.5 percent

The two items within reach in practice are subscription savings and the electronic contract. Number of children and household type are not things you arrange; these two are decided by a choice at the contract stage. Settling on an electronic contract before signing attaches 0.1 points.

Preferential rates apply only when documented. Believing you qualify carries no weight without paperwork, and both eligibility and size are finalised during screening.

Where the application starts and by when it must be filed

The application route changed once, so following old guidance wastes a trip. Today there are two paths: the fund's online channel or a handling bank. Applications through the Korea Housing Finance Corporation website were discontinued after August 2023.

  1. Enter consultation details - log in with a joint or financial certificate and fill in the required and optional fields
  2. Telephone consultation - a call follows on the basis of what was entered, with document guidance
  3. Submit documents - upload through the website, the mobile app or the chat channel
  4. Screening and approval - income, assets, collateral and non-ownership are verified. The asset review runs separately
  5. Visit the bank and receive funds - the loan agreement and mortgage registration papers are completed, then disbursement follows

The documents typically requested during screening are as follows.

  • Spouse's identification and consent to information provision
  • Resident registration abstract - to confirm household composition and every member
  • Family relationship certificate - to confirm marriage and children
  • Income and employment verification documents - varying by income type
  • Copy of the purchase or pre-sale contract
  • Copy of the lease contract - where a tenancy exists on the collateral home

Timing carries a hard deadline. The rule is to apply before the transfer of ownership is registered, and if registration is already done, within three months of the registration filing date. Miss that window and the application cannot be made at all, however well every other condition fits. Work backwards from the balance-payment and registration schedule together.

Where someone has applied for Didimdol and Bogeumjari at the same time and wants to drop one, the guidance describes cancelling both products and reapplying. Getting the order wrong pushes the schedule back.

Duties that follow disbursement - residency and staying at one home

Receiving the money is not the end. Didimdol carries conditions that continue after release, and this is where it diverges sharply from a commercial bank mortgage.

  • Residency duty - you must actually live in the collateral home. Renting it out while living elsewhere contradicts the premise
  • One-home duty - applied to new applications from 19 June 2024. Acquiring an additional home after the loan creates a problem
  • Failing these can trigger loss of the benefit of the term, requiring repayment of the loan
  • Withdrawal from the loan contract is possible only within a set window. A change of mind after release cannot be undone at will
  • Term, grace period and repayment method are difficult to change after release. The arithmetic has to be finished at application

Because of the residency duty, Didimdol may not fit anyone planning to switch homes or relocate soon. Conversely, if the purchase is a place to settle in, the duty is not a real burden. Deciding your living plan first and choosing the product second is the right order.

Where applications actually stall

Even with the paperwork in order, the places where progress stops are fairly predictable.

  • Net assets over the line - a jeonse deposit plus a vehicle pushing past 511 million won. Nobody knows until they calculate
  • A household member owning a home - a co-resident parent or adult child registers. Every member must own nothing
  • Pre-sale rights and occupancy rights - rights the applicant never thought of as housing can count as ownership
  • Floor area over the limit - above 85 square metres there is no route, however low the price
  • Appraisal differing from contract price - the contract sits under 500 million won but the valuation basis lands elsewhere
  • Documented income differing from perceived income - frequent for self-employed and freelance applicants. Low declared income cuts the limit sharply
  • Existing debt pushing DTI up - credit loans and instalments register larger than expected
  • Missing the application window - past three months from the ownership registration filing date, no application is possible
  • The nature of the collateral - registration as a neighbourhood facility, an illegal building record or senior rights all halt progress
  • Missing documentation for preferential rates - you qualified but had nothing to prove it

The two hardest to reverse on that list are floor area and the application deadline. The rest leave room for preparation; those two have no remedy once the moment passes. That is precisely why they belong before the contract.

Didimdol or Bogeumjari - line them up row by row

The two products differ from the operator down. Didimdol runs on the Housing and Urban Fund, Bogeumjari on the Korea Housing Finance Corporation, and their requirements are maintained separately. Laid out that way, the decision points come into focus.

  • If all four gates fall inside Didimdol, the roughly two-point rate gap is reason enough to check that side first
  • If net assets or floor area take you out, Didimdol drops away. Neither condition exists in Bogeumjari, which is where most people part ways
  • Anyone who already owns a home cannot use Didimdol, which requires every household member to own nothing
  • If you need more than 320 million won, the Didimdol ceiling may fall short
  • If you want a term of 40 years or more, Didimdol stops at 30
  • If you do not intend to live there, Didimdol is the wrong product from the outset. The duty runs past disbursement
  • Households with a newborn have a separate track, the newborn special Didimdol loan, whose income, limit and rate criteria differ from the general product described here and need checking on their own

Both products revise their requirements often, so which one suits you has to be rechecked against the guidance current on your application date. Didimdol runs on Housing and Urban Fund and MyHome portal guidance; Bogeumjari runs on the corporation's.

What this article could not pin down

Some items are not stated numerically here because they were not confirmed in official guidance. If they apply to you, check the fund's own material.

  • The detailed list of assets and debts entering the net-asset calculation - the 511 million won line is confirmed, but the valuation method for each item has to be read in the asset review guidance
  • Early repayment fee rates and exemptions - the 0.2 point rate benefit for repaying 40 percent or more is confirmed, but the fee structure needs separate checking
  • Tiers of the subscription savings reduction by holding period - only the 0.5 point cap is confirmed; the per-tier breakdown is not
  • Current treatment under DSR rules - policy loans have been handled differently at different times, so this must be checked as at the application date
  • The length of the residency duty and its recognised exceptions - the duty is confirmed; the period and exemptions are not
  • Current income and limit criteria for the newborn special Didimdol loan - only its existence as a separate product is confirmed here
  • The practical difference between the fixed rate and the five-year reset option - both exist, but the selection conditions and whether switching is possible need checking
  • The method for appraising the collateral home - the price basis may differ between apartments and other housing types

These bear directly on eligibility and amounts, so filling them with guesses derails a plan. Confirming through 1566-9009 or the asset review line on 1551-3119 is the accurate route.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Is income combined even if only one spouse earns?

Yes. Married applicants are assessed on the couple basis even where a spouse has no income; unmarried applicants are assessed alone. Note separately that if the spouse owns a home, the requirement that no household member owns property fails, so income and ownership must be checked as two distinct questions.

Q. How is the 511 million won net-asset figure calculated?

Total assets minus debt, at household level. Jeonse deposits, vehicles, financial assets and real estate all count as assets. Debt is deducted but only within a recognised scope, so it cannot be inflated to fit. The threshold moves each year and the review runs as a separate procedure, so anyone near the line should confirm through 1551-3119.

Q. An 84 square metre apartment qualifies but a larger one does not?

The test is 85 square metres or less of exclusive area, so the 84 square metre range, known locally as 34 pyeong, falls inside. The next size up mostly exceeds 85 and drops out. The key point is that the measure is exclusive area rather than supply area, and eup and myeon districts outside the capital region are allowed up to 100 square metres.

Q. I already signed and paid the balance. Can I still apply?

Guidance describes applications as possible within three months of the ownership registration filing date. The rule is to apply before registration, so anyone who has already registered should check how much of that window remains. Past three months, no application can be made however well the other conditions fit.

Q. I read the rate is 2.85 percent. Will I get that?

2.85 percent is the lowest cell in the grid: combined income of 20 million won or less on a ten-year term. It rises with income bracket and term, topping out at 4.15 percent for a thirty-year term. Homes outside the capital region receive 0.2 points off, and newlywed households use a separate schedule. Your own rate emerges only once bracket, term and preferential eligibility are entered together.

In summary - five things to check before applying

Didimdol has clear conditions, and being one notch outside any of them removes you from scope. In order, they run as follows.

  1. Income - which bracket the combined pre-tax figure lands in. This decides not just eligibility but the rate bracket too
  2. Net assets - whether the total, jeonse deposit and vehicle included, is 511 million won or less. Nobody knows until they calculate
  3. The home - whether it is both 85 square metres or less and appraised at 500 million won or less. Both must hold
  4. Limit calculation - the smallest of product ceiling, LTV, DTI and the amount you actually need, and whether clearing existing debt improves it
  5. Timing - whether you are inside the registration-based window, and how current the information you are reading actually is

The fifth matters most in practice. These figures move with policy, and the net-asset line shifts every year. Plenty of articles written on last year's basis still rank near the top of search results. Facing a decision as hard to unwind as a purchase contract, opening the official guidance once and reading it directly is the surest step available.
