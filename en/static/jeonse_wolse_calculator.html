📌 What is the Jeonse-Wolse conversion rate?

It is the annual interest rate applied when converting a Jeonse deposit into monthly rent (Wolse), or vice versa. Korea's Housing Lease Protection Act caps this rate, and as of 2025 the legal ceiling is the lower of the base rate (3.0%) + 2%p = 5.0% or 10% per year.
