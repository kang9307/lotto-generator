FAQ

How precise are the conversion results?

Length, weight, temperature, area, and volume are all computed in your browser using fixed conversion factors, and results are rounded to 10 significant digits for display. That is plenty for everyday use; for precision measurement work, double-check the raw figures and rounding.

Does it convert currencies?

No. This tool covers only physical units whose definitions never change. Currency conversion needs live exchange rates, so please use a dedicated currency converter for that.

Are the values I enter sent to a server?

No. Every calculation runs in JavaScript inside your browser, and the page's code contains no network request that transmits your input.
