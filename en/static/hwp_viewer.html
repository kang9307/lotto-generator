What «never transmitted» means
This page downloads a document-parsing program (WebAssembly) and runs it on your own machine. The document stays in browser memory and never goes over the network. Close the tab and it is gone.
It may look different from the original
Hangul documents use Hamchorom Batang and Hamchorom Dotum, fonts licensed for use inside Hancom Office only, so they cannot be served from this page. Substitute open fonts (Noto Serif KR, Pretendard) are used instead, which means line breaks and page boundaries can shift. When you need the exact original, open it in Hancom Office or the official Hancom viewer.

What it does

  • Opens .hwp (Hangul 5.0+ binary) and .hwpx (open format)
  • Page-by-page navigation, zoom
  • Renders documents containing tables, images and equations

What it does not do

  • Editing and saving are not supported. Read-only.
  • Password-protected documents cannot be opened.
  • Line-break differences caused by font substitution cannot be removed.

Editing was left out on purpose, not for lack of capability. Hangul files can be rejected by Hancom with a «file is damaged» error when they are rewritten wholesale. Reading only means your original is never altered.

Common questions

Why is the first open slow?
The parsing engine is about 7 MB. It downloads once on your first file and is cached afterwards.

Can I open company documents?
Nothing is transmitted, so this is safer than upload-based converters. Your own workplace policy on external web tools is a separate question.

Does it work on mobile?
Any recent browser works. It downloads 7 MB and renders pages, so slower devices will take longer.
