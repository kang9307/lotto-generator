📚 Basis and cautions

See the formula
Daily average wage = total wage of last 3 months / days in that period Daily allowance = daily average wage x 60% -> raised to the floor, or reduced to the cap Benefit days = age at separation x employment insurance period (120-270) Total payout = daily allowance x benefit days
  • 2026 floor of 66,048 KRW: derived from the 2026 minimum hourly wage of 10,320 KRW x 80% x 8 standard daily hours. Part time workers with fewer than 8 contracted hours a day get a proportionally lower floor.
  • 2026 cap of 68,100 KRW: the daily ceiling set by notice of the Ministry of Employment and Labor. High earners cannot be paid above it.
  • The floor and cap change every year with the minimum wage and ministry notices. Check the amount that applies on your separation date on the employment insurance site.
  • Monthly input mode converts to a daily average wage as monthly wage x 3 / 90. The law divides by the actual calendar days of the last 3 months (89 to 92), so the real figure can differ slightly.
  • Benefit days source: Schedule 1 of the Employment Insurance Act, applied as published. Up to 240 days under age 50 and up to 270 days at 50 or over or for disabled workers.
  • Not included: reductions for repeat claims, the early reemployment allowance, the unpaid waiting period, extensions of the claim window, and special rules for daily and self employed workers.
  • Tax: the job seeking allowance is not subject to income tax, but national health insurance contributions may still be charged during the claim period.
Eligibility requirements (separate from the calculation)

This calculator does not judge the requirements below. Eligibility itself is reviewed by the employment center.

  • 180 days of insured unit period: within the 18 months before separation (24 months for very short time workers), the days that formed the basis for paid remuneration must total at least 180. This is not simply days on the payroll.
  • Involuntary separation: redundancy, recommended resignation, expiry of a fixed term contract and business closure are typical cases. Voluntary resignation is in principle excluded.
  • Exceptions for voluntary leaving: unpaid wages, workplace harassment, a workplace relocation that makes commuting impractical, or illness of the worker or a family member may be accepted as justifiable reasons. Each case is judged individually.
  • Willingness and ability to work, plus active job seeking reported at every unemployment recognition date.

Confirm your own situation with the employment center for your area or the Ministry of Employment and Labor counselling line (1350 within Korea).

🚫 Warning about fraudulent claims · Hiding employment or part time work, or reporting false job seeking activity, is treated as fraud. It can lead to full repayment plus additional collection of up to five times the amount, suspension of payment, and criminal penalties (up to 3 years imprisonment or a fine of up to 30 million KRW under the Employment Insurance Act). Report any income during the claim period to the employment center immediately. Voluntary disclosure can reduce the sanction.
