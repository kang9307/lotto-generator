Bogeumjari Loan - Eligibility Criteria in Plain Terms

Who qualifies, how much actually comes out, and where it stalls - as of August 2026

2026-08-06 Finance & Economy

Read this first - the reference date and the limits of this article

This article reflects information as of August 2026 and does not substitute for a loan decision or an eligibility ruling. The Bogeumjari Loan is a policy mortgage available to residents of Korea. Its income thresholds, housing price ceiling, loan limits, interest rates and preferential items are adjusted frequently in line with policy direction and market conditions. Whether a specific person qualifies, and how much would actually be disbursed, cannot be settled by this article. Those outcomes rest with the Korea Housing Finance Corporation and the handling bank. Before applying, check the current product guidance and the published rate table directly at hf.go.kr. Nothing here recommends taking on a loan; this is material for your own judgment.

Where this product diverges from an ordinary bank mortgage

The Bogeumjari Loan is not something a bank sells out of its own funds. It is a policy mortgage supplied by the Korea Housing Finance Corporation, and the bank serves as the counter. That structure produces two consequences. One is that eligibility requirements exist at all. The other is that the interest rate is fixed to maturity.

An ordinary bank mortgage in Korea carries no income cap and no property price cap. In exchange, a large share of that market is floating rate, and even hybrid products typically reset after about five years. The Bogeumjari Loan runs the other way. Combined household income has to sit below a line and the collateral home has to sit below a price, but once you clear both, the rate fixed at the outset holds for ten years or for fifty. Monthly payments do not move when market rates rise. The same fact cuts the other way: they do not fall when market rates drop.

There are three purposes as well. Purchase funds for buying a home, preservation funds secured against a home you already own, used for purposes such as returning a tenant deposit, and repayment funds for paying off an existing mortgage. Most people think only of the purchase case, but refinancing an existing mortgage into this product is a common reason for looking it up.

If the general landscape of mortgage types and repayment methods is what you actually need, start with the Complete Mortgage Loan Guide. This article stays on one policy mortgage and one question: how Bogeumjari eligibility is decided.

Screening yourself through three gates first

The requirements look scattered, but they collapse into three groups. Walking them in order gets you to an answer quickly.

  1. Person requirements - Are you a Korean national of legal adult age, is combined household income below the threshold, and does your credit score clear the floor?
  2. Property requirements - Is the registered price of the collateral home 600 million won or less, and does the property fall within an eligible housing type?
  3. Ownership requirements - Do you own no home, or only one home aside from the property being pledged?

Most applications fail at the income line in gate one and the price line in gate two. In the capital region especially, the 600 million won property ceiling functions as the effective first filter. Gate three exists to screen out multiple-home owners, and most people already know where they stand on it.

The credit requirement sits separately. The corporation's guidance sets the floor at a CB score of 271 or higher. That is a low threshold within the usual Korean credit scoring range, so most applicants never touch it, but a history of arrears or debt restructuring is filtered out here first.

Clearing all three gates means you may fall within scope. It is not approval. Whether the loan is extended, and at what amount, is decided in screening by the corporation and the bank.

How far income counts, and where it gets cut

The income standard is not a single number. It splits by household composition. As of August 2026 the corporation's guidance sets out the following bands.

CategoryCombined annual household incomeNotes
General70 million won or lessThe baseline line
Newlywed couple85 million won or lessWithin 7 years of marriage registration
One minor child90 million won or lessBands split by number of children
Multi-child household100 million won or lessThe widest band

A few points matter when reading that table.

  • The basis is combined spousal income. A married applicant is assessed on the couple's figures even if the spouse earns nothing, while an unmarried applicant is assessed on their own income alone
  • The newlywed band runs from the marriage registration date, not the wedding date and not the start of cohabitation
  • Child-based bands count minor children. A child who has reached legal adulthood drops out of the calculation
  • The detailed split between one child and the multi-child band, and whether multi-child means two or three, can be applied differently in the income table and the preferential rate table, so it needs confirming in the corporation's own guidance
  • Income is generally read before tax, at the figure that appears on the supporting documents. Calculating from take-home pay and concluding you have room will put you off course
  • Employment income, business income and pension income each carry different documentation and different assessment methods. For freelancers and the self-employed the basis is declared income, which can diverge sharply from expectations

The awkward case in practice is income that sits just above a threshold. Do not settle that yourself. State your income type and documentation form to the corporation's call center at 1688-8114 and get it confirmed.

What the property itself has to satisfy

Clearing the personal side means nothing if the property fails. The property conditions are comparatively simple.

  • Price: a registered residential property at 600 million won or less
  • Type: apartments and other housing, meaning row houses, multi-household units and detached houses
  • Registration: it must be confirmed as housing on the register. Units recorded on the building ledger as neighborhood living facilities, the so-called geunsaeng villa, and officetels that are not classified as housing, fall outside scope
  • Title and encumbrances: senior rights or disputes that impair collateral value will stop the application in screening

One misunderstanding recurs around the 600 million won line. The transaction price and the assessed value are not the same thing. A contract written at 590 million won can still fail if the price reference the corporation relies on comes out differently. How that price is confirmed also differs between an apartment and a detached house. Skip this check and you can discover the property is out of scope only after the deposit is paid.

Housing type also feeds into the limit. Non-apartment housing carries a lower LTV ceiling. At the same price, a row house, multi-household unit or detached house yields a smaller loan.

How much comes out - the limit is the smallest of three numbers

This is where expectations diverge most. The headline ceiling and the amount that actually appears are different things. The final figure is the smallest of the following three, each calculated separately.

  1. Product ceiling - the maximum set for your category
  2. LTV figure - collateral property price multiplied by the applicable LTV ratio
  3. DTI figure - the amount at which annual principal and interest stays within the ratio to income
ItemAs of August 2026What to watch
General maximum360 million wonThe ceiling that applies to most applicants
Multi-child household / jeonse fraud victim400 million wonRequires documentation of the status
First-time homebuyer420 million wonFirst-time status is itself a separate test
LTV (apartment)up to 70%Based on collateral property price
LTV (other housing)up to 65%Row, multi-household and detached are set lower
LTV (first-time buyer, no prior home)up to 80%The uplift does not apply in the capital region or regulated zones
DTIup to 60%Payments on existing debt are counted in

Put numbers on it and the shape becomes clear. If the apartment costs 500 million won and LTV of 70 percent applies, the LTV figure is 350 million won. That is below the 360 million won product ceiling, so the working cap at that point is 350 million won. Run DTI next, and if annual principal and interest exceeds 60 percent against income, the figure comes down further. Reasoning that the home is 600 million won so 420 million won will come out does not hold.

The item most often missed inside DTI is existing debt. Credit loans, car installments and other secured loans enter the calculation. Taking out a large credit loan shortly before applying shrinks the mortgage limit by that much. Conversely, clearing a loan with a small remaining balance in advance can improve the calculation.

To gauge the limit in advance, the corporation's estimated loan inquiry tool is the better starting point. Treat the output as a reference figure; the approved amount can differ after screening.

How the rate is set - the fixed part and the 1.0 point of preference

The corporation publishes rates monthly. The rate posted at the time of application holds to maturity. On the table published 1 August 2026, the range by product and maturity is as follows.

Product10-year maturity50-year maturityCharacteristics

Preferential rates attach on top. There are several items, but stacking is capped at 1.0 percentage point. Qualifying for three does not add up without limit.

  • Low-income young borrower - annual income of 70 million won or less and under 40 years of age: 0.1 points
  • Newlywed household - within 7 years of marriage registration: 0.3 points
  • Multi-child household - two children 0.5 points, three or more 0.7 points
  • Socially considered groups - single-parent, disabled and multicultural households: 0.7 points
  • Jeonse fraud victims - up to 1.0 point

Two things matter in practice when reading rates. First, longer maturities carry higher rates; the gap between ten years and fifty years is 0.3 points. Second, preferential rates apply only when documented. Believing you qualify does not move the number if the paperwork does not establish it, and whether an item applies is settled during screening.

The fixed-rate character has two faces as well. It defends you while rates climb and leaves you comparatively worse off while they fall. Switching out at that point brings prepayment costs into play, which the next section covers.

Choosing a maturity and a repayment method

Loan terms run 10, 15, 20, 30, 40 or 50 years. The 40-year and 50-year options are not open to everyone; separate requirements attach. There are three repayment methods.

  • Equal principal and interest - the monthly payment stays the same. Predictable early burden, and the most widely used option
  • Equal principal - principal is divided evenly and interest accrues on the balance. The heaviest early burden, declining over time, with the lowest total interest
  • Graduated repayment - small payments early, rising over time. A candidate when income is expected to grow

One practical point carries real weight here. Once the loan is disbursed, the term, the grace period and the repayment method cannot be changed. Certain changes are possible before approval and at some stages after the transfer confirmation notice, but they lock at execution. This decision fixes a monthly outflow for decades, so it deserves the arithmetic at application time.

Prepayment penalties are worth checking too. The Bogeumjari Loan uses a sliding scale that decays as time passes. The structure is prepaid principal multiplied by the fee rate multiplied by (3 years minus elapsed term) divided by 3 years, so the burden disappears after three years. In addition, applicants who qualify on the application date as socially considered groups, multi-child households with three or more children, or jeonse fraud victims are exempt from the early repayment fee.

Where the application starts and what to prepare

This is not a product you begin at a bank counter. You start on the corporation's website and reach the bank at the end. The sequence runs as follows.

  1. Enter consultation details - log in with a joint certificate, financial certificate or simple authentication, then complete the required and optional fields
  2. Phone consultation - a consultation follows based on what you entered
  3. Submit documents - through the website, the Smart Housing Finance app, or the KakaoTalk channel
  4. Screening and approval - income, collateral, ownership status and credit are all verified
  5. Bank visit and disbursement - the agreement is executed at your chosen branch

Documents typically requested during screening include the following.

  • Identification for the applicant and the spouse
  • Resident registration copy - to confirm household composition and address
  • Family relation certificate - to confirm marriage and children
  • Income documentation - withholding receipts, income amount certificates and similar, depending on income type
  • Copy of the real estate contract - for purchase-purpose applications
  • Additional evidence supporting any preferential rate item you are claiming

Application timing carries deadlines as well. Purchase-purpose applications must be filed within a window tied to the transfer of ownership registration, and preservation and repayment purposes each have their own reference points. Miss the window and the application cannot proceed even when every other condition fits. Line this up against your contract schedule.

Getting the sequence wrong and visiting a bank first is a common detour. The online application comes first and the bank comes last. That said, some variants such as t-Bogeumjari are handled at the branch counter, so decide which route you are taking before you start.

Where applications actually stall in screening

Even when the paperwork lines up, progress tends to stop at a predictable set of places.

  • A property price near the line - if whether it clears 600 million won is settled only after the contract, there is no way back
  • A gap between documented and actual income - common for the self-employed and freelancers. Low declared income shrinks the limit sharply
  • Existing debt pushing DTI up - credit loans and installment payments count for more than people expect
  • Ownership determination - presale rights, occupancy rights and inherited shares can count as ownership even when the applicant does not think of them that way
  • The single-home disposal condition - proceeding while holding one home besides the collateral can attach a disposal undertaking, with consequences if it is not met
  • Non-apartment housing types - the lower LTV opens a gap against the expected amount
  • Missed application deadline - passing the window measured from the registration filing date or a similar reference makes application impossible
  • Credit issues - arrears history or debt restructuring is filtered at the CB score floor
  • Encumbrances on the collateral - senior rights, tenant issues or a violation notation on the building record will halt the process
  • Missing evidence for preferential rates - the item does not apply where documentation is absent, even if the applicant qualifies in substance

The two hardest to reverse on that list are property price and application deadline. The rest leave room for preparation; those two leave none once the moment passes. That is precisely why they belong before the contract, not after.

Didimdol or Bogeumjari - the numbers at the fork

The two products differ from the operator upward. The Didimdol Loan runs on the Housing and Urban Fund; Bogeumjari runs through the Korea Housing Finance Corporation. Their criteria are managed separately. As a rule, Didimdol is narrower and cheaper.

Set against each other, the decision points sharpen.

  • If income, net assets, property price and floor area all sit inside Didimdol, the rate gap alone is reason to check that product first
  • If any single one is exceeded, Didimdol drops out. Net assets and floor area are conditions Bogeumjari does not impose, and they are where many applicants part ways
  • If you already own one home, Didimdol's requirement that every household member own no home cannot be met
  • If the amount you need exceeds 320 million won, the Didimdol ceiling may fall short
  • If you are looking at a home larger than 85 square meters, Didimdol is out of reach even at a low price

Both products have their criteria revised often, so which one favors you needs rechecking against the official guidance at the time you apply. Didimdol is governed by Housing and Urban Fund guidance, Bogeumjari by the corporation's.

If you arrived by searching for the Special Bogeumjari Loan

If your search results mix in conditions such as no income cap, a 900 million won property ceiling or a 500 million won limit, those belong to the Special Bogeumjari Loan. They are not current.

  • The Special Bogeumjari Loan was a temporary product operated during 2023
  • It closed on 29 January 2024, and from 30 January the standing Bogeumjari Loan replaced it
  • After the change the criteria returned to pre-special levels. Income of 70 million won or less and a property price of 600 million won or less is the baseline
  • In exchange, the income bands for newlyweds and households with children were widened

A great deal of writing from 2023 and early 2024 still sits high in search results. Carrying those numbers into a plan throws the limit calculation off by more than 100 million won. Checking the year and reference date before reading anything else is the habit that protects you.

What this article could not pin down

Some items were not confirmed in official guidance, so no figure is stated here. If any of them bears on your case, ask the corporation directly.

  • The exact prepayment fee rate - the calculation structure and the exemptions are confirmed, but the applicable rate needs checking with the corporation
  • The available range of grace periods - it is confirmed that the choice cannot be changed after execution, but the selectable range needs separate confirmation
  • Detailed requirements for 40-year and 50-year maturities - age and child conditions are indicated but the specific thresholds need checking
  • Current treatment of DSR for this product - how policy mortgages are handled under DSR has shifted over time, so confirm it as of your application date
  • The exact split of income bands by number of children - the boundary between two and three or more children may be drawn differently in the income table and the preferential rate table
  • Whether income requirements are relaxed for jeonse fraud victims - the higher limit and the rate preference are confirmed, but how the income test applies needs separate confirmation
  • How the collateral property price is assessed - the price reference may differ between apartments and other housing types
  • How regional regulatory tightening affects this product - capital region and regulated zone criteria are sensitive to policy change

These all bear directly on the amount, so filling them in by guesswork will misalign your plan. Confirming through 1688-8114 or the product disclosure document on the corporation's site is the accurate route.

Frequently asked questions

Q. If only one spouse earns, is it still assessed as combined income?

Yes. A married applicant is assessed on the couple's combined figures even when the spouse has no income. An unmarried applicant is assessed on their own income alone. If a spouse begins earning, the combined result changes, so anyone near a threshold should calculate on the situation as it stands at application.

Q. I already own a home. Can I still apply?

The corporation's guidance describes the target as applicants who own no home, or who own one home aside from the property being pledged. Proceeding as a single-home owner can attach conditions relating to disposal, and how presale rights or occupancy rights count toward the ownership tally needs individual confirmation. Whether you fall within scope is determined in the corporation's screening.

Q. My property is a villa rather than an apartment. Do the terms change?

Row houses, multi-household units and detached houses are within eligible housing types. However, other housing carries an LTV of 65 percent, lower than the apartment figure, so the same price yields a smaller loan. Units recorded on the building ledger as neighborhood living facilities are not recognized as housing and cannot serve as collateral.

Q. Can I refinance an existing mortgage into this product?

A repayment purpose exists, so the structure allows it. The income, price and ownership requirements apply in full to a refinance, and the prepayment penalty on the existing loan has to be weighed against the new rate before any benefit appears. If your current rate sits below the published rate today, switching can leave you worse off.

Q. Will the amount shown in the estimated loan inquiry be the amount I get?

Treat it as a reference. The inquiry computes from what you entered, while actual screening reflects how income is documented, existing loan balances, the collateral valuation and your credit standing. Coming out lower than the inquiry is more common in practice, so setting a contract amount to match the inquiry result is risky.

Summary - five things to confirm before applying

The Bogeumjari Loan has clear conditions, and being off by one notch on any of them puts you outside scope. In order, the checks run as follows.

  1. Income - which band the combined pre-tax figure falls into, and what amount appears on the supporting documents
  2. Price and type - whether it sits at 600 million won or less, whether it is an apartment or other housing, and whether it is confirmed as housing on the register
  3. Ownership status - no home, or one home besides the collateral, and whether presale or occupancy rights get counted
  4. Limit calculation - the smallest of the product ceiling, the LTV figure and the DTI figure, and whether clearing existing debt improves it
  5. Timing - whether you fall inside the application window, and what date the information in front of you refers to

The fifth is the one that matters most in practice. The numbers in this scheme move with policy, and search results mix in conditions from a product that no longer exists. With a decision as hard to unwind as a purchase contract in front of you, one direct look at the official guidance is the surest step available.
