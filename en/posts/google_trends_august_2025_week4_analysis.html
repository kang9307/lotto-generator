4th Week of August 2025: When the Olympics and Back-to-School Season Create a Unique Search Pattern!

The Korean national team's achievements at the Paris Olympics collided with a back-to-school preparation rush, producing a truly distinctive search pattern. Here's a complete analysis of the trends from the last week of August -- where summer ends and fall begins!

The 4th week of August 2025 (August 25-31) was a remarkably special week for South Korean internet users. News of Team Korea's achievements at the Paris Olympics coincided with a back-to-school preparation frenzy, creating a unique landscape where sports and education became the two dominant pillars driving search trends. The tail end of summer vacations and the start of fall-season preparations also emerged as significant search themes.
