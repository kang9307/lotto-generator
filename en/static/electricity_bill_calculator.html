Tier boundaries

  • Normal months: tier 1 up to 200 kWh, tier 2 up to 400 kWh, tier 3 above 400 kWh
  • Summer, 1 July to 31 August: tier 1 up to 300 kWh, tier 2 up to 450 kWh, tier 3 above 450 kWh
  • Super user rate: applies only to usage above 1,000 kWh in summer (July and August) and winter (December to February)

Other unit charges

  • Climate and environment charge: ₩9.0 per kWh
  • Fuel cost adjustment: ₩+5.0 per kWh, reset quarterly within a range of plus or minus 5 won and currently held at the cap
  • VAT: 10 percent of the electricity charge subtotal, rounded to the nearest 10 won
  • Power industry infrastructure fund: 2.7 percent of the subtotal, truncated to 10 won, in force since July 2025
Calculation formula
  1. Energy charge = sum of usage in each tier multiplied by that tier rate, truncated to whole won. Tiers fill from the lowest upward, so the top rate never applies to your entire usage.
  2. Climate charge = usage × ₩9.0
  3. Fuel cost adjustment = usage × ₩5.0
  4. Base charge = the base charge of the highest tier reached, per household
  5. Electricity charge subtotal = base + energy + climate + fuel
  6. Power industry fund = subtotal × 2.7 percent, truncated to 10 won
  7. VAT = subtotal × 10 percent, rounded to the nearest 10 won
  8. Total billed = subtotal + fund + VAT, truncated to the nearest 10 won

Example, low voltage 350 kWh in a normal month: energy charge 200×120.0 + 150×214.6 = ₩56,190, base ₩1,600, climate ₩3,150, fuel ₩1,750, subtotal ₩62,690, VAT ₩6,270, fund ₩1,690, total ₩70,650.

What is not included

The items below depend on household circumstances or personal records, so they are excluded. Your actual invoice may differ.

  • Welfare discounts for disability, national merit, basic livelihood and near poverty households
  • Large family, multi child and newborn household discounts
  • Minimum usage guarantee deduction, abolished for ordinary households in July 2022 and kept only for vulnerable groups
  • Energy cashback and other savings incentives
  • Television licence fee, collected by KEPCO as a separate line item
  • Optional seasonal or time of use residential plans and smart meter tariffs
  • Common area electricity that an apartment management office allocates by its own rules
  • Late payment penalties and pro rata billing when the period is shorter than a full month
