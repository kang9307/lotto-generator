Mega Millions Game Information

How to Win

Match all 5 white balls + Mega Ball to win the jackpot. There are 9 ways to win prizes!

Drawing Schedule

Drawings are held every Tuesday and Friday at 11:00 PM ET.

Ticket Price

$2 per play. Megaplier adds $1 for multiplied non-jackpot prizes.

Odds of Winning

Jackpot odds: 1 in 302,575,350. Overall odds: 1 in 24.

How to Play Mega Millions

  1. Pick 5 numbers from 1 to 70 for the white balls
  2. Pick 1 number from 1 to 25 for the golden Mega Ball
  3. Choose Megaplier (optional) to multiply non-jackpot prizes
  4. Check the drawing on Tuesday or Friday nights
  5. Match numbers to win prizes from $2 to the jackpot

Prize Structure

Jackpot: Match 5 + Mega Ball

$1 Million: Match 5 white balls

$10,000: Match 4 + Mega Ball

$500: Match 4 white balls

$200: Match 3 + Mega Ball

$10: Match 3 white balls

$10: Match 2 + Mega Ball

$4: Match 1 + Mega Ball

$2: Match Mega Ball only
