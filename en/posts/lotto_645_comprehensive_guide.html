What Is Lotto 6/45?

Lotto 6/45 (Lotto yukosao) is the most popular lottery game in South Korea. Players choose 6 numbers from 1 to 45, and prizes are awarded based on how many of those numbers match the winning numbers drawn each week.

Lotto 6/45 Basic Facts

  • Draw day: Every Saturday at 8:35 PM (KST)
  • Ticket sales cutoff: 6:00 PM on draw day
  • Price: 1,000 KRW per game (up to 5 games per slip)
  • Operator: Dong-Haeng Bokgwon (Korea Lottery Commission)

How to Buy Lotto Tickets

In-Store Purchase

  • Where: Authorized Lotto retailers nationwide
  • Hours: Daily 6:00 AM - 12:00 AM
  • How: Fill out a marking slip or select automatic picks
  • Advantages: Instant purchase, cash payment accepted

Online Purchase

  • Website: Dong-Haeng Bokgwon official site (dhlottery.co.kr)
  • Hours: Daily 6:00 AM - 12:00 AM
  • How: Register an account and buy online
  • Advantages: Buy from home; prizes deposited automatically

Claiming Prizes and Taxes

How to Claim Your Winnings

  • 5th Prize (5,000 KRW): Claim immediately at any Lotto retailer
  • 4th Prize (50,000 KRW): Claim immediately at any Lotto retailer
  • 3rd Prize and above: Claim at the NongHyup Bank headquarters or branches
  • Claim deadline: Within 1 year from the draw date
Tax Notice
For prizes exceeding 50,000 KRW, a 22% income tax is automatically deducted. (An additional 2.2% local income tax applies separately.)

Tax Calculation Examples

  • 1st Prize of 2 billion KRW: Approximately 1.516 billion KRW after tax
  • 2nd Prize of 50 million KRW: Approximately 37.8 million KRW after tax
  • 3rd Prize of 1.5 million KRW: Approximately 1.13 million KRW after tax

Tips for Buying Lotto

  • Avoid birthday-only combinations: Picking only numbers 1-31 means you may have to share the jackpot with more winners
  • Avoid consecutive numbers: Sequences like 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 are chosen by many people
  • Spread your picks: Choose numbers evenly across the full 1-45 range
  • Balance odd and even: Aim for roughly 3 odd and 3 even numbers
  • Use the auto-pick option: The computer generates completely random selections

Important Reminders and Responsible Play

Important Disclaimer
The lottery should be treated as a form of entertainment, not as an investment or wealth-building strategy. Never spend more than you can afford, and keep it within a budget that does not strain your household finances.
  • Minors cannot purchase: You must be 18 or older to buy lottery tickets
  • Set a spending limit: It is recommended to spend no more than 1% of your monthly income
  • Prevent addiction: Only buy within a pre-set budget
  • Manage your winnings wisely: If you win, seek professional financial advice

Conclusion

Lotto 6/45 is a lottery game that anyone can easily participate in, but the odds of winning are extremely low. Think of it as a small source of hope and entertainment, and always play responsibly.

Winning would be wonderful, but even without a jackpot, the experience itself can be a small pleasure. Let us all contribute to a healthy lottery culture.
