📖 Cron Special-Character Guide

Learn what the special characters used in Crontab mean.

*

Asterisk (*)

Meaning: All values (wildcard)

Example: * * * * * = every minute

,

Comma (,)

Meaning: List of multiple values

Example: 0 9,17 * * * = 9 AM and 5 PM

-

Hyphen (-)

Meaning: Range

Example: 0 9 * * 1-5 = weekdays at 9 AM

/

Slash (/)

Meaning: Step / interval

Example: */15 * * * * = every 15 minutes

?

Question Mark (?)

Meaning: No specific value (in some implementations)

Example: Used in the day-of-month or day-of-week field

L

L (Last)

Meaning: Last (extended cron)

Example: 0 0 L * * = last day of every month

📦 Backup Job Scheduling

Nightly database backup at 2 AM

0 2 * * * /usr/local/bin/backup_db.sh

Runs the database backup script at 2 AM every day.

Full system backup every weekend

0 1 * * 0 /usr/local/bin/full_backup.sh

Performs a full system backup at 1 AM every Sunday.

📊 System Monitoring

System health check every 5 minutes

*/5 * * * * /usr/local/bin/system_check.sh

Monitors system status at 5-minute intervals.

Hourly log analysis

0 * * * * /usr/local/bin/analyze_logs.sh

Analyzes logs every hour on the hour to detect anomalies.

🔧 System Maintenance

Weekly system update, Tuesday at 3 AM

0 3 * * 2 /usr/bin/apt update && /usr/bin/apt upgrade -y

Updates system packages in the early hours of every Tuesday.

Monthly temp file cleanup on the 1st

0 4 1 * * /usr/local/bin/cleanup_temp.sh

Cleans up temporary files at 4 AM on the first day of every month.

📈 Report Generation

Daily report, weekdays at 9 AM

0 9 * * 1-5 /usr/local/bin/daily_report.sh

Generates the daily business report automatically at 9 AM on weekdays.

Monthly report on the last Friday

0 17 * * 5 [ $(date +\%m) -ne $(date -d '+7 days' +\%m) ] && /usr/local/bin/monthly_report.sh

Generates the monthly report at 5 PM on the last Friday of each month.

🧹 Cleanup Jobs

Daily log rotation at midnight

0 0 * * * /usr/sbin/logrotate /etc/logrotate.conf

Rotates log files at midnight every day.

Weekend disk cleanup

0 2 * * 6,0 /usr/local/bin/disk_cleanup.sh

Frees up disk space by removing unneeded files at 2 AM on weekends.
