7 Essential Supplements for Menopause (Gaengnyeongi): A Guide to Navigating the Change Healthily

Hot flashes, insomnia, mood swings... a supplement guide to naturally relieve menopause symptoms

Jun 24, 2025 Health & Wellness

Menopause (gaengnyeongi in Korean) typically starts in the late 40s - have you ever felt like you were going through it alone? It is a natural process experienced by countless women worldwide, yet its symptoms are anything but trivial. Sudden hot flashes, sleepless nights caused by insomnia, emotional roller coasters... enduring all of this on your own can feel overwhelming.

Today, we introduce seven essential supplements that can help you navigate menopause healthily and naturally. Rather than simply listing supplement names, we explain why each one matters, how it helps, and what to look for when choosing a product.

7 Essential Menopause Supplements

1. Isoflavone (Isopeulabon)

A natural plant-based estrogen extracted from soybeans that helps replenish declining female hormones naturally.

Key Benefits:

  • Relieves hot flashes and night sweats
  • Improves sleep quality
  • Stabilizes mood swings
  • Supports cardiovascular health

Recommended Dosage:

40-80 mg per day (soy isoflavone equivalent)

2. Calcium

The essential nutrient for protecting rapidly declining bone density after menopause. It helps prevent the bone loss caused by decreased estrogen levels.

Key Benefits:

  • Prevents osteoporosis
  • Maintains muscle function
  • Supports nerve transmission
  • Normalizes blood clotting

Recommended Dosage:

1,200 mg per day (for women over 50)

Tips to Boost Calcium Absorption

  • Take it together with vitamin D
  • Consume magnesium in a 2:1 calcium-to-magnesium ratio
  • Take after meals rather than on an empty stomach
  • Split doses to 500 mg or less per serving

3. Vitamin D

The essential partner that aids calcium absorption, while also boosting immunity and helping to alleviate depression.

Key Benefits:

  • Enhances calcium absorption
  • Strengthens immune function
  • Helps relieve depression
  • Maintains muscle strength

Recommended Dosage:

1,000-2,000 IU per day

4. Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Essential fatty acids that fight inflammation and protect cardiovascular health - a must for women going through menopause.

Key Benefits:

  • Reduces cardiovascular disease risk
  • Suppresses inflammatory responses
  • Improves cognitive function
  • Supports joint health

Recommended Dosage:

1-2 g per day (EPA + DHA combined)

5. Magnesium

An outstanding mineral for stress relief and sleep improvement, helping to ease the anxiety and insomnia that often accompany menopause.

Key Benefits:

  • Improves sleep quality
  • Regulates stress hormones
  • Relaxes muscles
  • Stabilizes heart rhythm

Recommended Dosage:

320 mg per day (for adult women)

6. Vitamin E

A potent antioxidant that protects cells from aging and helps maintain hormonal balance.

Key Benefits:

  • Relieves hot flash symptoms
  • Improves skin health
  • Provides antioxidant protection
  • Improves blood circulation

Recommended Dosage:

400-800 IU per day

7. Vitamin B Complex

Supports energy metabolism and nervous system health, effectively easing the fatigue and mood changes of menopause.

Key Benefits:

  • Supports energy production
  • Maintains nervous system health
  • Stabilizes mood
  • Improves cognitive function

Recommended Dosage:

Follow product-specific guidelines (B1, B2, B6, B12, etc.)

Important Precautions

  • Consult your doctor: If you are taking other medications or have a pre-existing condition, always talk to your physician first.
  • Start gradually: Don't begin all supplements at once. Add them one at a time and observe how your body responds.
  • Check quality: Choose products from reputable brands and read ingredient labels carefully.
  • Get regular checkups: Pair supplement use with routine health screenings.

Additional Tips for a Smoother Menopause

Lifestyle Improvement Guide

  • Exercise regularly: 30+ minutes of aerobic exercise, 3-4 times per week
  • Eat a balanced diet: Focus on whole foods like legumes, nuts, and fish
  • Get enough sleep: Aim for 7-8 hours of quality rest
  • Manage stress: Try meditation, yoga, hobbies, and other relaxation techniques
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol: These can worsen menopause symptoms

Final Thoughts: You Are Not Alone

Menopause marks the beginning of a new chapter in life. While it can be a challenging time, with proper nutrition management and lifestyle changes, you can absolutely enjoy a healthy and vibrant menopause.

We hope the seven essential supplements introduced today will be a steadfast companion on your menopause journey. Above all, value your body and mind, and never hesitate to seek professional help when you need it.
