1. What Is Urusa?

Basic Information

  • Active ingredient: Ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA)
  • Category: Liver function enhancer
  • Manufacturer: Daewoong Pharmaceutical (Dong-A Pharmaceutical)
  • Approval year: 1982

Key Benefits

  • Improvement of liver function
  • Promotion of bile acid secretion
  • Dissolution of cholesterol gallstones
  • Protection of liver cells (hepatocytes)

Urusa is a liver function enhancer whose active ingredient is ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA). Originally discovered in bear bile, this natural bile acid is now produced through chemical synthesis.

Since its introduction in South Korea in 1982, Urusa has been a flagship liver health medication for over 40 years and is widely used around the world to treat various liver conditions.

4. Side Effects and Precautions

Key Side Effects

Gastrointestinal Side Effects

  • Diarrhea (most common, 5-10%)
  • Abdominal pain and discomfort
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Indigestion

Neurological Side Effects

  • Headache (1-3%)
  • Dizziness
  • Fatigue
  • Insomnia (rare)

Other Side Effects

  • Rash and itching
  • Hair loss (very rare)
  • Muscle pain
  • Joint pain

4.1 How to Handle Side Effects

Managing Mild Side Effects

  • Diarrhea: Stay well hydrated; take the medication with meals
  • Abdominal pain: Take it 30 minutes after eating; consider dose adjustment
  • Headache: Get adequate rest and manage stress
  • Indigestion: Avoid greasy foods; eat smaller meals more frequently

When to See a Doctor Immediately

  • Severe or persistent diarrhea
  • Rash or other allergic reactions
  • Worsening or persistent abdominal pain
  • Worsening of existing symptoms

5. Dosage Instructions and Precautions

Proper Dosage Guidelines

When to Take It

  • 30 minutes after meals: Minimizes stomach irritation
  • At consistent times: Maintains stable blood levels
  • With plenty of water: At least 200 mL of water
  • Do not chew: Swallow the tablet whole

Contraindications

  • Acute cholecystitis: May worsen inflammation
  • Bile duct obstruction: May worsen bile stasis
  • Severe cirrhosis: Requires medical consultation
  • Pregnancy/breastfeeding: Contraindicated under the approved label

6. Dosage Guidelines for Special Populations

Children

  • Caution: Consultation with a pediatrician required
  • Monitoring: Regular liver function tests

During Pregnancy/Breastfeeding

  • Pregnancy: Contraindicated under the approved label
  • Breastfeeding: Possible transfer to breast milk

Older Adults

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How long should I take Urusa?

Generally, effectiveness is evaluated after 3-6 months of use. If liver function improvement is confirmed, the treatment duration is determined in consultation with your doctor. Long-term use may be necessary for chronic liver conditions.

Q2. Are there any medications I should not take with Urusa?

Antacids and cholestyramine can interfere with the absorption of Urusa, so they should be taken several hours apart. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist when combining Urusa with other medications.

Q3. Can I drink alcohol while taking Urusa?

Alcohol places a burden on the liver, so it is best to abstain while taking Urusa. If avoiding alcohol entirely is not possible, limit consumption as much as you can and get regular liver function tests.

Personal note: I quit drinking completely when I started taking Urusa. It was tough at first, but seeing my liver enzymes return to normal after three months made it all worthwhile. If it's for your health, you can absolutely do it!

Q4. Can I stop taking Urusa suddenly?

Stopping Urusa abruptly does not cause serious withdrawal effects. However, to maintain the therapeutic benefits, it is best to consult your doctor and taper off gradually.

Emergency Response Guide

Seek immediate medical attention if you experience any of the following:

  • Jaundice (yellowing of the eyes or skin)
  • Severe allergic reactions (rash, difficulty breathing, etc.)
