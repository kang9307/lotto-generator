Severance Pay Calculator

Enter your hire date, leave date and recent wages to see the daily average wage and your estimated severance pay (before tax) broken down step by step. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

⚠️ This is an estimate. The tool applies the standard formula of the Korean Employee Retirement Benefit Security Act for reference only. The amount actually paid can differ based on company rules, your employment contract, work rules and decisions by labor authorities. All figures are before tax, with retirement income tax excluded. Laws and administrative interpretations change, so check the current text and a qualified professional before making decisions.

See the formula and its legal basis

This calculator applies the formula exactly as written below.

Continuous service = leave date − hire date Calculation window = 3 months before the leave date (leave date excluded) Wage total = 3-month wages + annual bonus × 3/12 + leave allowance × 3/12 Daily average wage = wage total ÷ calendar days in the window Applied daily wage = max(daily average wage, daily ordinary wage) Severance pay = applied daily wage × 30 days × (service days ÷ 365)
  • 30 days per year of service: Article 8(1) of the Employee Retirement Benefit Security Act requires at least 30 days of average wage for each year of continuous service.
  • Definition of average wage: Article 2(1)6 of the Labor Standards Act defines it as the total wage paid in the three months before the triggering event divided by the total days of that period. Those are calendar days, not working days, so the divisor swings between 89 and 92 depending on which months you leave in.
  • Ordinary wage floor: Article 2(2) of the Labor Standards Act says that if the average wage falls below the ordinary wage, the ordinary wage is treated as the average wage. This tool compares them only when you enter an ordinary wage.
  • Bonus at 3/12: Ministry of Employment and Labor guidance divides the bonus paid over the 12 months before leaving by 12 and adds three months worth to the wage total.
  • Annual leave allowance at 3/12: 3/12 of the allowance paid for leave that was earned in the previous year and left unused is included. The allowance that only arises because of the resignation is excluded. This tool treats the amount you type as already filtered to the includable portion.
  • Eligibility: You need both at least one year of continuous service and at least 15 contractual working hours per week averaged over four weeks. The working-hour test depends on individual rosters, so the tool does not judge it automatically.
  • Not included: retirement income tax, social insurance settlement, DC or IRP investment returns, restart of the service clock after an interim settlement, and periods excluded from the average wage such as parental leave or industrial accident leave. If any of these apply, your real figure will differ.

The statutory text and the formula were cross-checked against the Korean Law Information Center, the Easy Law public legal service and the guidance shipped with the official Ministry of Employment and Labor calculator.
