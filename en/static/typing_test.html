FAQ

How are WPM and accuracy calculated?

WPM divides your correctly typed characters by five to get a word count, then divides by elapsed minutes. Accuracy is the share of correct keystrokes out of everything you typed, and mistakes are counted as errors.

Why is my score different from other typing sites?

Sites differ in text sets, word-count conventions, and how mistakes are handled, and your device, keyboard, and browser input latency also affect the measurement. Track your progress on the same site with the same device for meaningful comparisons.

Are my keystrokes or results sent to a server?

No. The whole test runs inside your browser, and the page's code contains no network request that transmits what you type. Results leave the page only if you press a share button yourself.
