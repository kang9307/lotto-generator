If You Decided to Hold Long Term - Custody Structure and Korean Rules

In long-term holding the real risk is not price, it is losing access

2026-08-01 Finance & Economy

Introduction: the problem that remains after you decide to hold

This article does not tell anyone to buy, to sell, or to stay away. It covers one thing only: what a person who has already decided to hold for a long time needs to know next. So there are no prices here, no returns, no forecasts. There is no talk of which asset is good or how much to allocate.

It starts from one observation instead. What actually breaks long-term holders is usually not price. When the price falls the asset is still there and the decision is still yours. Losing access is a different category. The balance is still visible on the ledger and there is no way to move it. The asset did not disappear; the connection between you and the asset was cut. And that state has no undo button.

The longer the holding period, the quieter this risk grows. Devices fail, passwords are forgotten, people move house, accidents happen. Things that barely matter to someone trading day to day arrive almost certainly at least once for someone holding across a decade.

So this article covers two things. One is custody structure: who holds the key, and what that choice costs. The other is the rules: what can be verified in Korea, and what has not been verified and therefore must not be stated as fact. Basic concepts such as what the asset is or what a wallet is were already covered in the beginner guide to cryptocurrency investing and are not repeated here.

Disclaimer. This article is written for informational purposes only. It does not recommend buying, selling or holding any asset, and it is not investment, tax or legal advice. Virtual assets are an asset class where a total loss of principal is possible, and losses arising from self custody are generally not recoverable. All decisions and their consequences are the reader's own responsibility, and a qualified professional should be consulted before acting. The rules and tax content below are based on public sources confirmed as of August 2026 and describe the Korean system specifically. Other jurisdictions differ, and the rules may change.

1. Risk first - three different things

The word risk is used as one lump, but it actually contains three things with completely different natures. Without separating them, preparation goes to the wrong place.

The first is price movement. That the value swings widely is well known, and this article does not predict its direction or size. One point does matter for long-term holders though. Price movement persists throughout the waiting period, but it does not by itself remove the asset.

The second is regulatory change. Which services keep operating, which procedures become required, how reporting and taxation shift, are all outside a holder's control. This too does not remove the asset, but it changes how the asset is handled and what it costs to handle.

The third is the subject of this article: loss of access. The decisive difference from the first two is that it cannot be undone. Prices can rise again and rules can change again, but an asset whose key is gone stays exactly where it is and never moves.

  • Regulation leaves time to respond once you notice the change. Loss of access is already finished when you notice
  • Provider risk has a counterparty, so claims and procedures exist. A self custody loss has no counterparty
  • So the row a long-term holder should spend time on is the bottom one. The three above it are largely outside personal control
  • That is also why this article avoids prices and forecasts. It covers only what can be controlled

2. The structure of custody - who holds the key

Choosing a custody method is usually discussed as convenient versus safe, but that is not the real axis. The real axis is one question: who holds the key that actually moves the asset. Everything else follows from it.

When assets sit in a provider account, the balance on the screen is closer to a claim against that provider. The provider manages the key, and the user requests a transfer through login and identity verification. That is why a forgotten password can be undone by process, and it is also why a problem at the provider becomes the user's problem directly.

Self custody is the mirror image. Holding the key yourself makes third-party insolvency or account restriction irrelevant, and for exactly the same reason there is no longer anyone to undo a mistake. If this trade is not acknowledged and self custody is understood only as safer, the risk has not been removed. Its type has been changed, while the sense of safety went up.

  • A provider account keeps an undo path in exchange for carrying counterparty risk
  • Self custody removes counterparty risk in exchange for giving up the undo path
  • A multi-approval setup resists single mistakes, but the added design and maintenance create new failure points
  • Mixed placement can be a realistic choice, yet without a record of what sits where it turns into a management failure
  • This article does not tell anyone which method to choose. It only sets out what each method demands in return

3. What holding your own key actually means

There is a phrase that comes up whenever self custody is explained: you hold the key yourself. The phrase is accurate, but usually only half of it lands. The half that lands is that nobody can block your asset. The half that rarely lands is that nobody will block your mistake either.

Listed concretely, here is what disappears.

  • There is no password reset. If it is forgotten, the recovery path does not exist in the design
  • There is nobody to prove ownership to. No counter accepts an identity document
  • There is no transaction reversal. A transfer to the wrong address has no institution behind it to pull it back
  • There is no monitor watching for anomalies on your behalf. The moment you press approve, that is the final judgement
  • There is no support desk. The absence of anyone to ask lands hardest on the day something goes wrong

So choosing self custody means taking on the roles of security officer, backup officer and disaster recovery officer at once. If there is no intention to actually perform those roles, self custody is not a choice that reduces risk. It is a choice that relocates risk and then leaves it unmanaged.

One test. Ask this: after settling on a method today, if I do nothing at all for five years, will the recovery material still physically exist and will I still be able to remember where it is? If the answer is no, the method does not fit you. Long-term holding means long stretches of not managing anything, and most accidents happen inside that gap.

4. What a recovery phrase is - what the public spec defines

The most misunderstood item in self custody is the recovery phrase, the word list often called a seed phrase. What it is, and why it alone restores the assets, is written not in marketing material but in a published technical specification. The following was confirmed in the BIP-39 specification document.

BIP-39 : entropy size and word count
  ENT bits   checksum bits   total bits   words
  128        4               132          12
  160        5               165          15
  192        6               198          18
  224        7               231          21
  256        8               264          24

  wordlist size : 2048 words
  checksum      : first ENT/32 bits of SHA-256 of the entropy
  seed function : PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512
  password      : the mnemonic itself, normalized as NFKD
  salt          : the literal word mnemonic, plus the optional passphrase
  rounds        : 2048
  output        : 512 bits

Unpacked, the structure is this. First random data is generated. The leading bits of its hash are appended as a checksum to form a bit string, which is split into groups of eleven bits and mapped to positions in a list of 2,048 words. That is why 128 bits yields 12 words and 256 bits yields 24. The important part is that the words are only a human-transcribable representation of the random data.

From those words a seed value is derived, and the actual keys are derived from the seed. The specification states that this runs through a fixed function for 2,048 rounds to produce 512 bits. The conclusion follows directly. Given the word list, the same keys are recomputed. No device needed, a different application is fine, years later is fine. And for the same reason, anyone who holds that list can move the assets.

The specification also defines an optional extra passphrase. That value is appended to the salt when the seed is derived, so the same word list with a different passphrase produces an entirely different wallet. The specification notes that every passphrase produces a valid seed. In practice this has two faces. It adds one more layer of protection, and it also becomes the element that blocks access even with an intact word list if it is forgotten.

No example phrase appears in this article. The specification description above is enough to convey the format, and writing out a sample word sequence is a bad practice in itself. Even an example becomes a starting point for an incident once it is typed or copied somewhere. A recovery phrase is not created even as an example.

The storage rules do not come from the specification. They follow directly from the structure above. Because the word list is itself the control of the asset, it belongs only where that control may sit.

  • No photographs. A captured image is copied through the gallery, automatic backup, search indexing and device migration into places the owner cannot track
  • No cloud notes or documents. One breached account carries the whole holding. Synchronisation is a convenience feature and a distribution channel
  • No message to yourself, no email drafts folder. Chat backups and server retention leave copies
  • Putting it in a password manager deserves careful thought. If that application falls, everything falls at one point. Decide consciously what shares a single basket
  • Do not type it into any site or application to check it. The moment it is entered, what that screen does is unknowable
  • Two copies in different physical locations. So that one fire, flood or theft does not take both
  • Verify recovery once with a small amount before moving the bulk. Whether what you wrote down actually works is unknown until it is tried

5. How loss happens - four paths

The outcome called losing it is the same, but the paths differ. Different paths mean different things to do in advance. The four patterns that recur are set out below.

Forgotten password or passphraseLong disuse. Sometimes even the fact that a passphrase was set is forgottenRecord the existence and a hint separately from the value. Keep provider account recovery options currentProvider accounts have a process. Self custody usually does not
No inheritance preparationOnly the owner knows the holding exists. The family does not know what to look forWrite a keyless access note. Record storage locations and name someone to contactWithout preparation, effectively none
Phishing and impersonationContact posing as support, a screen offering to help recover, pressure to approve quicklyKeep the rule that the recovery phrase is never entered anywhere, without exceptionOnce the transfer completes, nothing
  • Among the four, the top two change character entirely with preparation. Prepared, the incident drops to an inconvenience
  • The third row is preparation or nothing. Almost nothing can be done afterwards
  • The fourth is defended less by preparation than by never making an exception to the rule. Phishing arrives carrying urgency and helpfulness together
  • They share one trait. Learning on the day of the incident is always too late. All of it is prepared in calm periods only
  • And provider accounts and self custody differ structurally in the room left to respond. How much sits on each side is therefore also a management question

6. Inheritance and incapacity - the asset only you know about

This is the item most often skipped in long-term holding. Deciding to hold for a long time also means the asset may outlast you, yet preparation is usually absent. Self custodied assets especially so, because no third-party register exists that anyone can query to confirm the holding the way a bank account or a property register can be.

What can be prepared splits into two layers. The first is the layer that says what exists, and the second is the layer about how to reach it. Separating the two is the point. The access note never contains the recovery material itself. If finding the note alone makes the assets immediately transferable, the preparation has become the vulnerability.

[ Access Note ]  keep this WITHOUT any key or recovery word

1. What exists
   - asset type          : ______
   - custody type        : provider account / self custody / both
   - service or device   : name only, no login, no PIN, no words

2. Where the recovery material is
   - location A          : ______  (physical place, sealed)
   - location B          : ______  (different building)
   - note                : this document never contains the words themselves

3. Who to contact
   - person to notify    : ______
   - professional        : tax advisor / lawyer : ______

4. What NOT to do
   - do not photograph the recovery words
   - do not type them into any site or app to check them
   - do not send them by message, mail, or cloud storage
   - do not act in a hurry because someone says it is urgent
  • Keep the access note and the recovery material in different places. Finding one must not enable an immediate transfer
  • Assets in a provider account are relatively better off because there is somewhere to ask. However, the documents and procedure may differ by provider and need to be confirmed
  • A self custodied holding without an access note is not even known to exist. In that case the outcome is the same as having nothing
  • Deciding who in the family to tell changes a great deal on its own. They do not need the whole picture, only that the note exists and where it is
  • When the holding changes, update the note. An outdated note can be more dangerous than none
  • Inheritance tax duties, valuation methods and the specific legal requirements are not asserted in this article. They are items to be confirmed, case by case, with qualified tax and legal professionals

7. The Korean rules - only what was verified

Rules change, so what follows records the source and the reference date together. What could not be verified is left marked as unverified. Note that this section describes Korea specifically; readers elsewhere should check their own jurisdiction.

Per Financial Services Commission material, the Act on the Protection of Virtual Asset Users took effect on 19 July 2024. Its core is separating user assets held by a provider from the provider's own property. User deposits are placed with or entrusted to a bank, may be operated only in safe assets such as government and municipal bonds, and are to be paid to users with priority even if the provider becomes insolvent. On the asset side there is a duty to keep at least 80 percent of the economic value of user virtual assets in wallets separated from the internet, and for the internet-connected portion, insurance or a reserve with a coverage limit of at least 5 percent is required. The minimum amounts are at least 3 billion won for won-denominated markets and at least 500 million won otherwise. Use of undisclosed material information and market manipulation carry criminal penalties and administrative fines, and providers must monitor abnormal transactions continuously and report them.

The anti-money-laundering side is in flux. According to Financial Services Commission material released on 30 March 2026, amendments to the enforcement decree and supervisory regulations concerning the reporting and use of specific financial transaction information were put out for public comment from 30 March to 11 May 2026. The content includes widening the scope of major shareholders subject to review beyond the largest shareholder, adding new requirements of a debt ratio of 200 percent or below and sound credit, expanding the information provision duty between domestic providers from the current threshold of one million won and above down to below one million won, requiring suspicious transaction reporting for overseas transactions at ten million won and above excluding low-risk cases, and clarifying the duty to verify the accuracy of information obtained in customer due diligence.

8. Tax - what was verified and what was not

Tax is an area where numbers matter, so only what was confirmed in National Tax Service guidance is written here. Where it could not be confirmed, no number is written. This describes Korean taxation; other jurisdictions differ.

According to National Tax Service guidance, following the December 2024 amendment to the Income Tax Act, transfers and lending on or after 1 January 2027 are taxed as other income under separate taxation. The minimum threshold that functions as a basic deduction is 2.5 million won per year and the rate is 20 percent. Reporting nets annual gains and losses and is filed as separately taxed other income during the comprehensive income tax period from 1 May to 31 May of the following year.

Non-residents are handled differently. The guidance states that income arising when a non-resident or foreign corporation transfers, lends or withdraws virtual assets in Korea is taxed as domestic source other income. In that case the virtual asset service provider withholds the tax and pays the amount withheld each month by the tenth day of the following month. The rate is the lower of 10 percent of the transfer value and 20 percent of the transfer value minus necessary expenses. Residents of a country with a tax treaty may submit an exemption application to the provider to have it applied.

9. Checklist - decide, duplicate, record

Turned into actions, everything above becomes the following. Work down the list.

  • [ ] The custody method was chosen explicitly. Not it happens to be there, but a decision
  • [ ] For each method, what it demands in return is understood (undo path versus counterparty risk)
  • [ ] If a provider account is used, the login recovery options are current
  • [ ] If self custody is used, recovery material exists in two copies in different physical locations
  • [ ] The recovery material is not in photos, cloud storage, notes apps or messengers
  • [ ] Recovery was verified with a small amount before moving the bulk of the holding
  • [ ] If an extra passphrase is used, its existence is recorded separately from the value
  • [ ] A keyless access note exists and is kept apart from the recovery material
  • [ ] Someone is designated to be told that the note exists and where
  • [ ] There is a method for recording acquisition dates, quantities and transfers
  • [ ] A date to review the rules and tax position again has been set
  • [ ] The rule that the recovery phrase is never entered anywhere has no exceptions
  • [ ] A principle was set that contact demanding an urgent approval stops the process first
  • [ ] When the holding changes, the access note gets updated

Conclusion: what is usually lost is access, not price

Once the decision to hold is made, what is needed next is not a forecast but structure. Decide who holds the key, accept what that choice demands, and prepare to carry it.

To summarise what was verified: entrusting assets to a provider keeps an undo path in exchange for counterparty risk. Korea has had rules in force since 19 July 2024 aimed at reducing that risk, with concrete duties such as deposit segregation and an offline storage ratio. Conversely, self custody removes counterparty risk and removes the undo path with it. Under the public specification, a recovery phrase is the value from which the keys are recomputed, so losing it and handing it to someone else lead to the same place.

And tax was confirmed to apply to transfers and lending on or after 1 January 2027. The practical implication for a long-term holder is not the rate but records. Acquisition dates and transfer history cannot be manufactured later.

There is one thing to do now. Ask whether anyone could reach this asset if you became unreachable today. If the answer is nobody, that is not a problem with the custody method. It is an item that has not been prepared yet. And it stays there regardless of what the price does.
