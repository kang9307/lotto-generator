    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    Which units can I convert?

    Temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin), 8 length units (mm, cm, m, km, inch, feet, yard, mile), 5 weight units (g, kg, oz, lbs, ton), 5 volume units (ml, l, cup, pint, gallon), 4 area units (square meters, pyeong, acre, hectare), and 4 speed units (km/h, mph, m/s, knot) are supported. Entering a value converts it to every unit in the same category at once.

    How precise are the results?

    Most units are rounded to 2 decimal places; km, mile, and acre are shown with 6, and gallon and hectare with 4. Conversions use standard factors such as 1 inch = 0.0254 m, 1 lbs = 453.592 g, and 1 pyeong = 3.305785 square meters.

    Where is my conversion history stored?

    The last 5 conversions are stored only in this browser via localStorage and are never sent to a server. Press the Clear History button or delete this site's browser data to remove them completely.
