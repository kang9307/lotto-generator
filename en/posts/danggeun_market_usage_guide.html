Before You Start: Age, Account, and Identity Verification

Daangn (also known internationally as Karrot) can be used by mobile phone users aged 14 or older under Korean age counting rules (full years). During sign-up the user must consent to a mandatory item confirming they are at least 14, and without that consent the account cannot be created. If a user states, directly or indirectly, that they are under 14, use can be restricted. Someone who really is 14 or older can appeal by submitting a carrier document through the inquiry form: SKT's contract registration certificate, LG U+'s subscription confirmation, or KT's original-record certificate. The restriction lasts until proof is submitted and is shown as up to 1,825 days.

The phone number works as the account ID, so one phone number can hold only one account. If the number changes, identity verification must be done again. If someone else registers with a number and the old account's phone verification expires, that account is deleted automatically after 60 days, so after a number change the new number should be verified within that window.

Selling carries a separate condition. Daangn's help center says that from July 21, 2026, following an amendment to the e-commerce act, phone identity verification is required in order to sell second-hand items. Anyone already verified does not need to repeat it, and without verification the selling function cannot be used. The path is My Daangn, then Settings (top right), then Account/Info Management, then Daangn identity verification, or the verification prompt inside the sales-post screen.

  • Age rule: 14 or older, with a mandatory consent item at sign-up.
  • Number of accounts: one account per phone number.
  • Selling condition: since July 21, 2026, selling second-hand items requires phone identity verification.
  • Verification prompts at login: they can be requested for accounts never verified, accounts verified more than a year ago, and logins from a new device.
  • Daangn Pay: a separate verification is required under current law, as noted in section 9.

When a dispute arises, a seller's identity information such as a phone number is provided to the buyer only if the seller agrees, and the guidance says the seller chooses whether to provide it. Knowing this before listing an item clarifies how far identity verification goes in terms of what is disclosed.

Neighborhood Setting and Verification: The 30-Day Expiry and Its Limits

Daangn's second-hand trading assumes local neighbors, so two separate steps exist. First, "Set My Neighborhood" decides which area to use. Then "neighborhood verification" confirms the user is physically in that area. Up to two neighborhoods can be chosen, and the guidance suggests places that are visited often, such as home, work, or school. Posts from the set neighborhoods and nearby areas are recommended in line with interests.

The verification procedure is short. Open "My Daangn" at the bottom of the app, tap the neighborhood verification button, use the icon at the lower right of the map to fetch the current location, and tap the button to complete verification. If the neighborhood to be verified differs from the actual location, verification fails. Verification can be obtained only when the user is actually in the neighborhood they want to access, and sharing a verification code with another person so that they verify on your behalf is strictly prohibited because of the risk of account takeover.

Verification expires after roughly 30 days. Leaving the device's GPS on lets the app verify automatically at launch, and a notification is sent one day before expiry. Once it has expired, what can and cannot be done splits as follows.

StateSend new chatsWrite new postsNotes
Verification validPossiblePossibleTrading only in the verified neighborhood
Expired (about 30 days without verifying)Not possibleNot possibleExisting chat rooms and chats a prospective buyer sent first remain usable
Neighborhood removed from My Neighborhoods after verifying——Verification may expire unless redone within 48 hours, and the one-day-before notice may not be sent

When verification fails, the official document names three causes. First, the device's location permission or the precise-location setting is off (on Android, Settings, Location, App permissions; on iPhone, Settings, Daangn, Location). Inside a building, in a basement, or while moving, the location may not be found, so the guidance advises trying again near a window or outdoors. Second, the neighborhood to verify differs from the actual location. Third, verifying in too many neighborhoods in a short time, or by abnormal methods, can lead to verification being restricted. If none of the three applies, an inquiry goes through My Daangn, then Customer Center.

  • To trade in another neighborhood, first change My Neighborhood and verify there. Once verified, chatting remains possible after leaving that area until the verification expires.
  • After moving, set the new neighborhood as My Neighborhood and verify. Earlier second-hand posts can be moved through My Daangn, the gear icon, and the option to change the neighborhood of second-hand posts. Only posts in the "for sale" state are moved, hidden and deleted posts are excluded, and every post written in that neighborhood changes at once. Neighborhood Life posts are not affected. There is no need to cancel the account because of a move.
  • The verification count shown on a profile is based on the last 30 days.

Finding Items and Price Offers: Search, Distance Filters, and Etiquette

Search starts from the magnifying-glass icon at the top right of the home screen. With two neighborhoods set, search results show a "near [neighborhood]" button that lets users view items around the chosen area. To see only nearby items, the "within 2 km" button keeps items whose seller is within 2 km. Switching the sort order from "recommended" to "nearest first" lists closer items first, and the two features can be combined. The "nearby neighborhoods" shortcut at the top of the home screen gathers posts from close areas only, but it may not appear when there are few new posts near the user's neighborhood even after verification.

The official document explains result ordering with three criteria. Relevance is how well a post matches the search intent, recency is the posting and bump date, and preference is user response such as clicks, likes, and chats. The home feed uses recency, interest relevance (view, like, and chat history), and post quality (whether the post is informative and thorough). The help page gives no exact scoring formula.

Price offers come with rules for both buyers and sellers. A post with a "make a price offer" button signals that the seller is willing to receive offers, and tapping it lets a buyer send a desired price. If the seller is interested, the seller sends a chat message, and no response should be read as a rejection. A post without the button means the seller does not want offers, and a "price offers not accepted" label appears at the top of the chat screen.

  • Do not make an offer unless there is a real intention to buy. A price far from the going rate gets no reply.
  • Repeatedly offering on a post that does not accept offers can bring a bad-manner rating, and as ratings accumulate the Manner Temperature drops and repeated cases can lead to restricted use of the service.
  • On posts without the offer indicator, asking for shipping costs to be included in the price, citing in-person meetup terms to demand a lower price, or asking for the item as a giveaway can bring a warning or a bad-manner rating even if the negotiation succeeds, according to the document.

Writing a Sales Post: Path, Category, Photos, and Display Area

A sales post is written from the "+ Write" (or "+") button on the home screen, then "Sell my item." Entering item information in the title and detailed description makes recommended categories appear automatically. The guidance says the more concrete the item name, use, and features, the more accurate the recommendation. Photos are attached from the camera icon, either from the album or by shooting immediately, and long-pressing a photo lets the order be changed. The sales-tips document (FAQ 200) says the first photo matters too, so shoot it to bring out the item's features, take several from different angles, and zoom in on parts that need explanation.

At the bottom of the writing screen, the "choose the neighborhood to show this post" control uses a bar to set the area in which the post is displayed. The help page does not explain the effect of this range in figures. FAQ 12 says trades are possible only in areas where neighborhood verification has been completed.

There is also a feature in which uploading only photos lets AI produce a draft and a recommended price. After adding images on the writing screen, a toggle for AI writing appears at the bottom and can be turned on or off, and if text typed in advance disappears because of AI writing, the restore button at the right of the title and description fields recovers it. Photos that clearly show what the item is, such as model name, brand name, product tag, or the box, raise accuracy. The official document states that AI can be wrong and that the seller is responsible for the posted item, so the model name, included accessories, and defect descriptions in the draft should be checked by hand before posting.

  • Use photos taken yourself. The operating policy says photos that are not of the actual item cause misunderstanding.
  • Wash or clean the item before selling. The guidance says a clean item improves the mood of a transaction even if it shows wear.
  • Do not write general or chatty posts unrelated to second-hand trading.
  • When reselling something bought on Daangn, state the reason for resale and sell only at a price equal to or lower than the purchase price.
  • Cash transactions (lending or borrowing money and the like), medicines and medical devices, overseas direct-purchase devices that have not passed domestic conformity assessment, and government-supported goods such as Narami rice or local gift certificates are described as prohibited. Prohibited items are also not allowed in giveaway or wanted posts.

Pricing Criteria: Checking the Going Rate and the Order of Adjustment

The pricing method in the official help converges on one thing. Search Daangn for similar items and take the prices of completed trades as the going rate. The key point is to look at completed posts, not at the asking prices of posts still on sale. FAQ 200 gives the prices of completed items as the way to see the going rate. For a quick sale, the guidance says to price slightly below the going rate. The official documents give no specific discount width (for example, a percentage), so the numbers below are only a worked example calculated for illustration.

  1. Search for similar items and collect only the prices of completed posts. Prioritize posts with the same model, accessories, and usage period.
  2. Look at the range of the collected prices. If completed prices cluster between 80,000 and 100,000 won, the area around the middle is the reference (calculated example).
  3. Subtract defects and missing accessories from the price and also write them in the description. Daangn dispute mediation is based on what the seller disclosed before the trade (section 6).
  4. When the item does not sell for a long time, lower the price and bump the post. Lowering the price sends a price-drop notification to neighbors who tapped like (section 7).
  5. The guidance says the sales assistant function suggests a discount price based on Daangn market prices at an appropriate time when it is used.

Whether to accept price offers is up to the seller. A post with an offer button means the seller will take offers, and for posts without it the operating policy (FAQ 41) lists, as buyer manners, not asking for a price discount. Because the AI-recommended price is described as based on the prices of similar items actually sold, it works best as a cross-check against a going rate researched by hand.

Reselling something bought on Daangn has its own standard. It can be sold only at a price equal to or lower than the purchase price. Resale aimed at making a margin does not fit that standard, and items received as a giveaway cannot be resold at all (section 7).

Description and Defect Disclosure: The Three-Level Standard

Daangn's manners guide says defects that were not stated are grounds for a refund. On the other hand, it also says that for items handed over in person, refunds for signs of use are difficult. Combining the two sentences gives the seller's description standard: signs of use need not be listed, but defects must be. What, then, counts as wear and what counts as a defect? Daangn's dispute mediation center says it proposes a mediation plan based on what the seller disclosed before the trade and on objectively verifiable evidence, and it divides condition into three levels.

LevelDefinitionElectronics exampleClothing and shoes example
Signs of useMarks that arise naturally from normal useTiny scratches on the rim, everyday marks on the backNatural pilling on knitwear, ordinary sole wear
Minor defectVisible damage but normal functionSmall crack at a screen corner (touch and display normal), sunken button (works normally)A tear of 1 to 2 cm, noticeable stains, broken button or zipper
Serious defectMalfunctioning or hard to useScreen unreadable, does not power onBadly torn clothing, shoe with a detached sole

The mediation center document says that announcing a minor defect beforehand in the description and photos makes the trade safer for both sides, and that a serious defect directly affects function or use and so must be announced before the trade. There is also a sentence saying buyers should accept signs of use. Translated into sales-post terms, it reads as follows.

  • One line is enough for signs of use. State the location and degree, as in "everyday scratches present."
  • Show a minor defect in a photo and also write it in words. The mediation center document says to announce it beforehand through the post description and photos.
  • A serious defect must be announced before the trade, the mediation center document says. Placing it at the start of the description is this article's suggestion.
  • Write usage period, purchase date, and accessories (box, charger, and so on) from the start, since buyers ask about them first.

The limits of mediation are also known. The document says odor defects are hard to verify objectively, and that a plant's growth potential or pests are hard to mediate because the time and cause of the problem cannot be known. Whether a perfume is genuine can be checked through expert opinion, so an application is possible. For processed food, selling past the expiry date or an item described as unopened that was opened are cases where an application is possible, while poor storage after receipt or spoilage of unknown cause are classed as hard cases. Separately, the operating policy (FAQ 41) says refunds over signs of use are hard after a trade bought in person, and explains that in Instant Buy (Safe Payment) trades the payment stays held by Daangn Pay if the parties do not agree, so when facts such as an item different from the post or a fake, missing components, or a defect with a major effect on use that was not disclosed or was understated are confirmed by objective materials, Daangn can refund or settle the trade on its own authority under a set procedure.

Reserving, Completing, Bumping, and Sharing

Trade status is changed in My Daangn, then Sales History, by tapping the three dots at the lower right of a post and choosing "change to reserved" or "change to completed." When an appointment is made, switch the post to reserved, and if someone has reserved, sell to that person. The operating policy says breaking a reservation destroys trust. A post in the completed or reserved state can be returned to on-sale from the same menu. FAQ 11 says a trade review can be sent from the completed list under Sales History or from Purchase History in My Daangn (section 8).

Bumping updates a post's registration time to the latest. A bumped post gets personalized recommendation on the Home tab, which is described as raising the chance of a sale. The path is My Daangn, Sales History, the three dots on the post, then Bump, or the Bump button under the post. There are three limits.

  • At most 6 posts per day can be bumped.
  • At most 15 bumps are allowed per post.
  • A bumped post can be bumped again only after a certain period has passed. The length of that period is not given as a number in the official documents.

If a post still has not sold after 15 bumps, the guidance says to wait a while and write the post anew. In the new post, the document recommends retaking photos so they show the item well, enriching the description, and adjusting the price sensibly. Lowering the price while bumping sends a notification to neighbors who tapped like, so because the number of bumps is limited, pairing bumps with price cuts is efficient. Since the waiting period has no stated figure, the time needed to use all 15 bumps is not calculated.

Sharing uses the same writing screen: entering 0 won as the price makes the post display automatically as a giveaway. Choosing "accept applications" opens a sharing event in which neighbors apply instead of chatting, and the giver picks from the applicants and starts the chat first. Sharing is not first come, first served, and conditions can be written in the post. A recipient cannot resell the item, and violations can lead to reports and sanctions. Posts that pretend to be giveaways while selling something or asking for an action are also against the operating policy. There is no cap on how many times someone can receive items. The official selling tips also suggest turning items that are hard to sell but still usable into giveaways.

Manner Temperature, Reviews, and Manner Ratings

Manner Temperature is a metric that starts every user at 36.5 degrees and can rise to a maximum of 99. Praise ratings and positive reviews raise it, while bad-manner ratings, negative reviews, and sanctions for operating-policy violations lower it. It can be checked on a profile, and the face changes to a smile as the temperature rises and to a frown as it falls. If a counterpart's temperature is below 36.5, the official document recommends checking the rate of wanting to trade again and the reviews carefully.

Time rules govern how the score moves. Ratings and reviews are not reflected immediately and can take about 7 to 10 days. The purpose is to keep it impossible to tell who left a negative rating. More recent ratings count for more, and ratings and reviews older than 3 years are no longer reflected. That is why the temperature can drift down slightly when activity is sparse. The documents list what feeds into the temperature. FAQ 135 gives users with frequent disputes or frequently broken appointments as examples whose temperature keeps falling, and FAQ 18 explains that old ratings count for less because trust is hard to judge when someone has been inactive for a long time.

Manner ratings and trade reviews are different tools. A manner rating is left on the counterpart's profile through "praise manners" or "three dots, then give a bad-manner rating." Praise ratings are visible to other users, while a bad-manner rating can be seen only by the person who received it, and who left it is handled anonymously. Bad-manner ratings that accumulate over a period can lead to suspension, so the guidance asks that they be left only when necessary. A bad-manner rating that is untrue can bring a warning as a false rating.

  • Cases of broken appointments to which a bad-manner rating applies: contact is lost after time and place were set, a unilateral cancellation just before the trade, not appearing at the meeting place.
  • A trade review is left through the Send Review button in My Daangn (Sales History for completed trades, or Purchase History) or the Send Review button at the top of the chat room. That button appears only 30 minutes after the chat started.
  • If a review request notification arrives although no trade took place, there is no penalty for not writing one.
  • The rate of wanting to trade again is calculated from trade reviews and is shown only after at least 5 reviews are received. Positive reviews such as "good" and "the best" raise it.

Daangn Pay: Fees and Limits for Transfers, Safe Payment, and Instant Buy

The amounts and limits in this section are the values written in Daangn's help center documents as of October 5, 2026. Fees and limits can change with policy updates, and discounts or events may apply. Before any actual payment or transfer, check the notice on the app screen and Daangn Pay's guidance. Nothing here encourages use or guarantees any particular amount.

Daangn Pay is joined from the guidance banner on the My Daangn tab. One account is allowed per person, and use starts at age 14 (a 14-year-old must have passed the birthday and use a phone in their own name). Identity verification is required separately from Daangn account verification. There are four main uses.

  • Daangn Money transfer: from the Daangn Pay button above the chat window or from plus, then send, enter the amount and a 6-digit password to complete. A transfer once sent cannot be canceled.
  • Bank account transfer: send to an account number from the account-transfer button in My Daangn or on the Daangn Pay home.
  • Sending to my own account: a bank account in the user's own name must first be linked to Daangn Pay and verified. Withdrawals are possible only to a linked account in the user's own name.
  • Safe Payment and Instant Buy: Daangn Pay holds the purchase amount and hands it to the seller after the purchase is confirmed.

Instant Buy combines Safe Payment with parcel pickup booking. When the seller turns on Instant Buy in the writing screen and enters the shipping origin and weight, a courier collects the parcel at the door and delivers it to the buyer's door. Delivery is handled by CJ Logistics, and address details are not exposed to each other. The conditions are app version 25.37.0 or later, an item price of 1 to 1,900,000 won, joining Daangn Pay, a weight of 20 kg or less, and a sum of three sides of 160 cm or less. Digital devices and home appliances are allowed only at 200,000 won or less, and it cannot be used for tickets and vouchers, wanted posts, or sharing posts. Large or irregular items, fresh food, plants, glass or liquids, items packed in shopping bags, and items at high risk of breakage cannot be accepted for parcel shipping.

The buyer must press purchase confirmation promptly after receiving and checking the item, and only then does the payment go to the seller. If it is not pressed for a certain period, it can be confirmed automatically under Daangn's criteria. If there is a problem, a dispute mediation request can be filed before purchase confirmation, and meanwhile Daangn Pay holds the payment. Damage after confirming too early depends on whether the case is eligible for Safe Compensation review, so separate guidance warns against confirming before receipt. Documents for a compensation application must be submitted within 7 days of the first request, and the review usually takes about 15 business days, according to the document.

Choosing a Trade Method and Handling Problems After the Trade

The trade method is decided by the two parties. There are two ways, meeting in person and not meeting (parcel or door-handle handover), and for trades without meeting, the guidance says using Instant Buy, Safe Payment, or parcel booking is safer and more convenient. The biggest difference between the two is that defects found after an in-person handover are hard for Daangn to help with. The operating policy section on meeting in person says it is good for the seller to demonstrate or try on the item when the buyer is curious how it works or wears, and that refunds over signs of use are hard after a trade bought in person. Anyone who does not want to reveal an account number can send a simple transfer through Daangn Pay.

Instant BuySafe Payment plus parcel booking3.3% (opening-period 2.2% also stated), shipping fee guided separatelyDispute mediation before purchase confirmation, settlement can be held

The documents also set out criteria for cancelling a trade. Before the item is handed over, both sides can cancel by agreement. Daangn does not step into reservation-deposit or down-payment matters made by agreement, nor into cases where someone demands travel costs or a penalty although no trade took place, nor into refund requests made long after a completed trade. Daangn helps only in a limited way with disputes over item condition after a trade has actually been completed. When exchanging a deposit, it is therefore better to leave the cancellation terms in the chat.

What Could Not Be Confirmed

  • The distance radius or administrative-unit scope that neighborhood verification recognizes: the help page says only that the user must actually be in the neighborhood and gives no distance figure.
  • The waiting time before a bump can be reused: it is written only as a certain period.
  • The face-expression thresholds of Manner Temperature and whether anything is restricted below 36.5: calculation details and band criteria are not in the published documents.
  • Daangn Money top-up and holding limits, and the full monthly transfer limit of Daangn Pay: only the Safe Payment limit and the ATM withdrawal limit were confirmed.
  • The Instant Buy shipping rate table and the seller-side settlement fee: only the 3.3% borne by the buyer was confirmed, and whether any seller fee exists could not be confirmed in these documents.
  • Which of 3.3% and the opening-period 2.2% for the Instant Buy usage fee applies now: two documents from the same point in time state different values.
  • How long a mistaken-transfer return takes and how often it succeeds: FAQ 8780 only says that getting the money back may be hard without the other party's consent, and gives no period or rate.
  • The body of the official blog's writing guide and the full text of the second-hand refund policy: only the help-center summaries were read.
  • Menu-position differences by Android and iPhone app version: some items in the documents say they may differ depending on the current version.
