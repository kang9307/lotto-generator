Match all 5 white balls + Powerball to win the jackpot. There are 9 ways to win prizes!

Odds of Winning

Jackpot odds: 1 in 292,201,338. Overall odds: 1 in 24.9.

How to Play Powerball

  1. Pick 5 numbers from 1 to 69 for the white balls

  4. Check the drawing on Monday, Wednesday, or Saturday nights
  5. Match numbers to win prizes from $4 to the jackpot
