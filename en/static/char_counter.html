❓ FAQ

Is my text stored or sent to a server?

No. All counting happens in real time inside your browser, and this page contains no code that saves your text or sends it anywhere. The text disappears when you close the page, so keep a copy of longer drafts elsewhere.

Why does the character count differ from other tools?

Services count spaces and line breaks differently. Here, the without-spaces figure excludes both spaces and line breaks, and some special characters such as emoji may internally count as two characters. When you need to meet a limit enforced by a specific system, treat that system's own counter as the authority.

How is the byte count calculated?

It uses UTF-8. Latin letters and digits take 1 byte each, while most Korean, Japanese, and Chinese characters take 3 bytes. Some systems use legacy encodings that count these characters as 2 bytes, so check the target system's rules when a byte limit applies.
