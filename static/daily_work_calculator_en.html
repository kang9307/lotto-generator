Frequently Asked Questions

What is a work unit? +
A work unit represents a day's work. 1 unit = full day, 0.5 unit = half day. This helps calculate pay for partial days worked.
Where is my data stored? +
All data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to any server - your information stays private on your device. However, clearing browser data will delete your records.
Are holidays shown automatically? +
Yes, US federal holidays from 2025-2028 are automatically highlighted in red on the calendar.
Can I manage multiple job sites? +
Yes, you can register multiple job sites with different daily rates, overtime/night multipliers, meal and transport allowances. Each work entry is linked to the currently selected site.
