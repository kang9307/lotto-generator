Frequently Asked Questions

What is a gongsu (work unit)? +
Gongsu is the unit used in Korean day labor to measure how much work was done in a day. 1 gongsu means a full day of work, and 0.5 gongsu means a half-day of work.
Where is my data stored? +
All data is stored only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to any server, so your personal information stays private and secure. However, if you clear your browser data, your records will be deleted along with it.
Are public holidays marked automatically? +
Yes. South Korean statutory public holidays and substitute holidays from 2025 through 2028 are automatically shown in red on the calendar.
Can I manage multiple work sites? +
Yes. You can register multiple work sites and set different daily wages, overtime and all-night multipliers, meal allowances, and transport allowances for each one. When you enter a day on the calendar, the calculation uses the settings of the currently selected site.
