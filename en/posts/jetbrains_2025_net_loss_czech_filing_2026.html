What the Headline About a JetBrains Net Loss Actually Covers

On October 6, 2026, GeekNews carried an item titled, in translation, "JetBrains: revenue up, but a swing to net loss in 2025." The page it links to is not a statement from the company. It is a company data page on Helgi Library. The summary on that page gives 2025 revenue of CZK 16,008 mil, a net loss of CZK 315 mil and a return on equity of -11.1%, and it describes the nature of the figures as "the company's own (unconsolidated) statutory statements filed with the Czech business register." In other words, these are the company's own unconsolidated statutory statements as filed with the Czech business register, read from the filings and converted from thousands of CZK into millions of CZK.

The subject of the headline therefore has to be read narrowly. The entity that recorded a net loss is not the whole group that operates under the JetBrains name. It is the standalone set of books of a single legal entity registered in Czechia, JetBrains s.r.o. In its company facts panel, Helgi Library gives the type of statements as "Standalone 2005–2025," the source as "Czech business register (Sbírka listin), individual financial statements," and the reporting basis as "CZK mil · Czech GAAP." No consolidated financial statements appear on that page.

Currency raises the same issue. The unit of the source table is CZK million. Amounts shown in dollars have passed through a conversion by Helgi Library, so the sections below read the figures in koruna and deal with the dollar amounts separately in one section. Every amount given below without a unit, including every cell of the tables, is in CZK million.

  • Whose figures these are. One legal entity, JetBrains s.r.o. (registration number 26502275).
  • Type of statements. Unconsolidated, standalone statutory financial statements.
  • Accounting standard and currency. Czech GAAP and the Czech koruna (CZK); the table is expressed in CZK million.
  • Who processed the data. Helgi Library read the figures from the register filings and arranged them into its own standardized line items. Every value below is a value as processed by Helgi Library.
  • Date of the data. The Helgi Library page shows October 6, 2026 as its last update.

JetBrains s.r.o. in the Czech Register — A Company Wholly Owned by a Dutch Entity

In the public register (Veřejný rejstřík) kept by the Czech Ministry of Justice, JetBrains s.r.o. is filed under section C, insert 86211, at the Municipal Court in Prague (Městský soud v Praze). The date of registration is January 2, 2002, the legal form is a limited liability company (společnost s ručením omezeným), and the registered seat is Na hřebenech II 1718/8 in Nusle, Prague 4. The "Founded 2002" and "Headquarters Praha" entries in the Helgi Library company facts panel agree with what the register records for this entity.

The shareholder (společník) section of the register contains a single entry. JetBrains N.V., with an address in Amsterdam and registration number 56460279, is the sole shareholder and has fully paid in a contribution of CZK 216,000, and the registered capital (základní kapitál) of the company is likewise CZK 216,000. This means JetBrains s.r.o. is a subsidiary whose entire ownership interest is held by a Dutch entity. That is where the qualification comes from that the standalone statements of the Czech entity do not represent the group as a whole. The profit or loss of the parent, JetBrains N.V., and of any other entities that may sit beneath it, is not included in these books.

The documents for fiscal year 2025 appear in the register's collection of documents (Sbírka listin) as two entries. One is a 45-page document that combines the financial statements, the annual report and the report on relations (účetní závěrka, výroční zpráva, zpráva o vztazích). The other is a three-page auditor's report (zpráva auditora). Both reached the court on July 30, 2026 and were placed in the collection on August 20, 2026. The document type is recorded as účetní závěrka, and no document in the list is labelled as consolidated financial statements.

  • Registration. January 2, 2002, Municipal Court in Prague, file C 86211.
  • Sole shareholder. JetBrains N.V. (Amsterdam), holding the entire ownership interest.
  • Registered capital. CZK 216,000.
  • Receipt of the 2025 financial statements. Reached the court on July 30, 2026 and entered the collection of documents on August 20, 2026.

Total Revenue rose in every one of the five years. The Revenue Growth row on Helgi Library reads 11.0%, 20.0%, 12.5%, 12.4% and 6.26%. The 2025 figure of CZK 16,008 million is the value Helgi Library calls "a record for the company," and the growth rate of 6.26% is the lowest of the five years.

The profit lines moved the other way. Gross Profit fell every year, from CZK 5,332 million in 2021 to CZK 1,708 million in 2025, and the Gross Margin row reads 53.7%, 42.7%, 35.2%, 18.8% and 10.7%. The amount left after subtracting Gross Profit from revenue grew from CZK 4,594 million to CZK 6,817 million, CZK 8,683 million, CZK 12,231 million and CZK 14,300 million (calculated). What the table shows is that the narrowing of profit at the operating level is not something that happened in 2025 alone; it runs across all five columns.

Even so, net income stayed positive through 2024. Net Income was CZK 3,971 million, CZK 2,290 million, CZK 3,856 million and CZK 2,479 million, and then CZK -315 million in 2025. EBIT (earnings before interest and taxes), the line that corresponds to operating profit, is still positive in 2025 at CZK 751 million. The point at which the sign changes is the row beneath EBIT, Net Financing Costs.

  • Gross Profit minus Operating Expenses matches EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization). For 2025, 1,708 - 790 = 918 (calculated).
  • EBITDA minus Depreciation & Amortization gives EBIT. For 2025, 918 - 167 = 751 (calculated).
  • Pre-Tax Income minus Income Tax gives Net Income. For 2025, -325 less -10.0 is -315 (calculated).
  • EBIT minus Net Financing Costs does not match Pre-Tax Income exactly. The gap is CZK -2 million in 2021, CZK 156 million in 2022, CZK 546 million in 2023, CZK 130 million in 2024 and CZK 2 million in 2025 (calculated). That means there are items between the two rows that the public table does not show.

Ratios and Rankings — How They Are Computed and How Helgi Library Classifies the Company

The return on equity (ROE) of -11.1% is a number Helgi Library places in the row of headline indicators at the top of the page, next to revenue and net profit. The summary says this value is 107 percentage points lower than a year earlier, which follows from the 2024 ROE of 96.0%. The ratio works out when it is computed on average equity and not on year-end equity. For 2025, CZK -315 million divided by CZK 2,849 million, the average of CZK 3,724 million and CZK 1,974 million, gives -11.1%; for 2024, CZK 2,479 million divided by CZK 2,582 million, the average of CZK 1,440 million and CZK 3,724 million, gives 96.0% (calculated). The ROE row reads 99.0%, 70.8%, 182% and 96.0% from 2021 onward. Those levels arise because the denominator, equity, ranged between CZK 1,440 million and CZK 3,724 million and was therefore of a similar size to net income in the same years. The Dividends Paid row reads CZK -4,157 million in 2021, CZK -3,209 million in 2022 and CZK -4,976 million in 2023.

The other ratios can likewise be recomputed from the amounts in the table. EBITDA Margin of 5.73% is CZK 918 million divided by CZK 16,008 million, EBIT Margin of 4.69% is CZK 751 million divided by CZK 16,008 million, Net Margin of -1.97% is CZK -315 million divided by CZK 16,008 million, and Net Debt to EBITDA of -8.26 times is CZK -7,584 million divided by CZK 918 million (calculated). This shows that the summary and the table come from the same set of books. It does not show that they have been checked against the original filing.

The ranking is assigned within Helgi Library's own classification. The site places JetBrains under "Media & Entertainment · Czechia," and its summary says the company ranks first by revenue and fifteenth by ROE among "the 15 media companies in Czechia." The objects of business listed in the Czech register include the provision of software, information technology consulting, data processing, hosting and web portals, and research and development in the natural and technical sciences. The phrase "first of 15 media companies in Czechia" is therefore a position inside one data site's classification table and not an industry statistic.

  • ROE can be recomputed on the basis of average equity, that is, the mean of equity at the end of the previous year and at the end of the year itself (calculated).
  • ROA (return on assets) of -1.71% and ROCE (return on capital employed) of 26.3% appear in the same table. The 2024 value in the ROCE row is 76.4%.
  • "Rank by size 1st of 15" is a revenue ranking inside Helgi Library's Media & Entertainment · Czechia group.
  • The summary gives the number of indicators as 721, while the Coverage line on the same page gives 730.

Figures Restated in Dollars — Converted Values and Back-Calculated Exchange Rates

The GeekNews summary states amounts in dollars. It notes that the amounts are Helgi Library's dollar conversions, rounded, and that the growth rates are based on the original filing currency, the Czech koruna. The Helgi Library page has a currency switch for CZK, EUR and USD. Setting the koruna values next to the dollar values shows that a different exchange rate has been applied to each year, as the table below sets out for revenue, operating profit and net profit or loss.

The 2024 values use roughly 23.2 koruna per dollar and the 2025 values roughly 21.9 (back-calculated by dividing each koruna value by the corresponding rounded dollar value). Comparing one dollar amount with another therefore produces a different growth rate. Revenue grew by 6.26% in koruna, but in the converted dollar values it appears to grow by about 12.6%, from about USD 649 million to about USD 731 million (calculated). The difference arises because the exchange rate used in the conversion was not the same in the two years; it does not mean that revenue grew by that much more. When changes are being described, the koruna values are the reference.

  • The dollar values originate in Helgi Library's conversion, and the GeekNews summary reproduced them in rounded form.
  • The implied rates are obtained by dividing by rounded dollar values, so they are meaningful only to one decimal place.
  • "A net loss of about 14.4 million dollars" and "a net loss of 315 million koruna" are two notations for the same value.

What belongs in the right-hand column cannot be filled in by recomputing the numbers in the table. The composition of net financing costs, the use of the investing outflow, and profit or loss at group level are questions answered by the notes, the annual report and consolidated financial statements respectively. Reading the direction of the business or the success or failure of its products out of the single fact that one standalone entity recorded a net loss in one year goes beyond the scope of this data.

  • The sentence "JetBrains made a loss" agrees with the data only when narrowed to "the 2025 standalone statements of JetBrains s.r.o. record a net loss of CZK 315 million."
  • "Operations are loss-making" does not agree with the table. EBITDA of CZK 918 million and EBIT of CZK 751 million are positive.
  • "There is CZK 7,584 million in cash" does not agree with the table either. Cash and equivalents are CZK 1,444 million, and CZK 7,584 million is a value taken from the Net Debt row.

What Could Not Be Verified

  • Composition of Net Financing Costs. The public table does not show which items make up the CZK 1,078 million recorded for 2025. The financial statements in the register's collection of documents are PDF documents and were not included among the sources.
  • The company's explanation. No official explanation from JetBrains of the 2025 net loss was found. That is why no cause of the loss is described here.
  • Consolidated group results. Neither the financial statements of the sole shareholder, JetBrains N.V., nor consolidated group figures are among the sources.
  • Comparison with the original filing. The values in the tables are as processed by Helgi Library, and they have not been matched line by line against the amounts in the financial statements filed with the register.
  • Helgi Library's line-item definitions. The public page does not show which Czech GAAP accounts are grouped into Gross Profit, Operating Expenses and Net Debt. Operating Expenses rose by 25.0%, from CZK 632 million to CZK 790 million (calculated), while the Operating Expenses Growth row reads 26.5%, so it is also unclear whether the two rows use the same basis.
  • Exchange rate behind the dollar conversion. The source and the reference date of the rate used by Helgi Library, and whether it is an annual average or a year-end rate, are not shown on the public page. The figures 23.2 and 21.9 are back-calculated.
  • Number of employees. The page title includes the word Employees, but the public table has no headcount row.
  • Use of the investing outflow. The public table does not show which assets the CZK -10,273 million was spent on acquiring.
  • Total Debt and Net Debt Issuance. Total Debt rose by CZK 1,642 million, from CZK 282 million to CZK 1,924 million (calculated), while the Net Debt Issuance row in the cash flow table reads CZK 52.5 million. The public page does not make clear whether the two rows measure the same thing.
