❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do the repayment methods (Equal Payment, Equal Principal, Bullet) differ?

Equal Payment repays the same amount (principal + interest) every month. Equal Principal repays a fixed principal each month plus interest on the remaining balance, so the payment gradually decreases. Bullet repayment pays the principal in one lump sum at maturity; this calculator computes its interest as simple interest (principal x annual rate x term). If you enter a grace period, only interest is paid during that period.

Are taxes applied to the results?

No. All results are pre-tax, and taxes such as interest income tax (15.4% standard in Korea) are not applied. The amount you actually receive depends on your tax type and the financial institution's terms, so use the results for reference only.

What does the compounding frequency mean?

It is how often interest is added to the principal. You can choose annually, semi-annually, quarterly, monthly, or daily; the shorter the period, the larger the maturity amount at the same annual rate. Additional monthly contributions are compounded monthly and added to the total.
