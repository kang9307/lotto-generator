❓ Game Guide

🃏 Memory Card Game

  • Goal: Match all card pairs.
  • How to play: Click cards to flip them and find matching number pairs.
  • Score: Faster completion time and fewer moves earn higher scores.
  • Tip: Remember card positions and look for patterns.

🔢 Math Game

  • Goal: Solve as many problems as possible within 60 seconds.
  • How to play: Enter the answer to each equation that appears on screen.
  • Score: 1 point per correct answer, with bonuses for consecutive correct answers.
  • Tip: Practice mental math for faster calculations.

⚡ Reaction Speed Test

  • Goal: React as quickly as possible when the signal changes.
  • How to play: Click immediately when the circle turns green.
  • Score: Shorter reaction times earn higher scores.
  • Tip: Stay focused, relax, and wait.

🏆 Achievements & Badges

  • First Game: Unlocked when you complete your first game.
  • 7-Day Streak: Visit the site 7 days in a row.
  • Memory Master: Complete the memory game in 15 seconds or less.
  • Math Whiz: Score 50 or more points in the math game.
  • Lightning Reflexes: Achieve a reaction time of 200ms or less.
