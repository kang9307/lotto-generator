How to Use
Lotto Info

Choose the number of games (1-20) and click the Generate Numbers button to draw numbers with a ball-by-ball animation. Numbers are picked from 1 to 45 without duplicates using your browser's cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues). In Semi-Auto mode you can set must-include numbers (up to 5) and excluded numbers (up to 6). Your last 10 results are stored only in your own browser.

Korea's Lotto 6/45 requires players to pick 6 numbers from 1 to 45. There are 8,145,060 possible combinations, so the odds of winning first prize are 1 in 8,145,060 — and every combination has the same odds. Drawings are held every Saturday, and you can check the winning numbers on the official Dhlottery website.

Honest Facts About the Odds

  • In the Korean-style Lotto 6/45 there are 8,145,060 possible combinations, so each game has a 1 in 8,145,060 chance of winning first prize. Note: other lotteries differ — Japan's Loto 6 draws 6 of 43 and China's Double Color Ball uses a different format — so these odds apply to the 6/45 format only.
  • Every combination has exactly the same odds, and past winning numbers have no influence on future draws (the gambler's fallacy). This tool is a random generator for fun — it does not and cannot improve your chances of winning.
  • The sum, odd/even ratio, and consecutive-number info shown with each result are for fun and reference only. They have no relation to winning.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the lottery numbers generated?

The generator uses your browser's cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) to pick 6 unique numbers from 1 to 45. Modulo bias is removed by rejection sampling, so every number has exactly the same chance. A plain random fallback is used only in unsupported browsers.

What are the odds of winning first prize?

In the Korean-style 6/45 format there are 8,145,060 possible combinations, so each game has a 1 in 8,145,060 chance of winning first prize. Japan's Loto 6 (6/43) and China's Double Color Ball use different formats with different odds.

Does this generator improve my chances of winning?

No. Every combination has exactly the same odds, and past winning numbers have no influence on future draws. This tool is simply a fun random generator that makes picking numbers easier.

Where are my generated numbers stored?

Your last 10 results are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server, and you can delete the history at any time with the Clear History button.

Is the lottery number generator free?

Yes, this is a completely free service. Anyone can use it — no sign-up or payment required.
