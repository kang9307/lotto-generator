Frequently Asked Questions

How are the Powerball numbers generated?

The tool uses the browser’s cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) to pick 5 white balls from 1-69 without duplicates and 1 Powerball from 1-26. Modulo bias is removed by rejection sampling, so every number has the same probability. A regular random fallback is used only in unsupported browsers.

What are the odds of winning the Powerball jackpot?

There are 11,238,513 white ball combinations (C(69,5)), multiplied by 26 Powerball numbers for a total of 292,201,338 combinations, so the jackpot odds are 1 in 292,201,338 per play.

Does this generator improve my chances of winning?

No. Every combination has exactly the same odds, and past results do not influence future drawings. This is a random picker for entertainment only.

Where are my generated numbers stored?

Your last 10 results are stored only in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server, and you can delete the history at any time with the clear button.
