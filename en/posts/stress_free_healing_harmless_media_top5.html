Why We Need Harmless Content Now More Than Ever

The modern person's day is packed with a relentless flood of information and sensationalized content. Scroll through social media for a few minutes and you'll inevitably encounter negative news, posts that fuel comparison and competition, and videos with clickbait thumbnails that quietly drain your mental energy.

Media Consumption in South Korea (2025)
Average daily media consumption: 8 hours 32 minutes
Average smartphone usage: 4 hours 48 minutes
Percentage who have experienced stress from negative content: 73.2%
* Source: Korea Press Foundation, 2025 Media Usage Survey

In this environment, "harmless content" becomes a true haven for the mind. Harmless content refers to healthy media that calms the soul and delivers positive energy without being overstimulating.

Today, we're sharing the top 5 harmless media picks for stressed-out modern people. Try dedicating just 30 minutes a day to this kind of content for a truly restorative break.

#1: Nature Documentaries - Healing Through the Screen

Why Nature Documentaries?

Nature documentaries deliver a healing experience that feels almost like being in the wild, thanks to stunning visuals and soothing narration. Scientific research has shown that simply watching nature footage can lower cortisol (the stress hormone) levels.

Recommended Nature Documentaries

1) Our Planet (Netflix)

  • Highlights: David Attenborough's soothing narration paired with breathtaking 4K cinematography
  • Recommended episode: "Frozen Worlds" - the beauty of the polar regions
  • Healing factor: The grandeur of nature puts everyday worries into perspective

2) Earth at Night (Apple TV+)

  • Highlights: Nighttime ecosystems captured with cutting-edge low-light technology
  • Why we love it: The tranquility of nature after dark brings deep calm
  • Healing factor: Gentle background music and mesmerizing footage also help with sleep

3) BBC Earth YouTube Channel

  • Highlights: Free, high-quality nature footage
  • Recommended playlist: "Calming Nature Scenes"
  • Healing factor: Offers everything from short clips to full-length features
Tips for Effective Viewing
Dim the lights and watch for 30 minutes after dinner
Put your phone away and fully immerse yourself
Watch an hour before bedtime to promote better sleep
Even better when watched with family or pets

#2: ASMR & White Noise - Peace for Your Ears

Why ASMR and White Noise?

ASMR (Autonomous Sensory Meridian Response) refers to the pleasant, relaxing sensory response triggered by certain auditory stimuli. White noise consists of consistent frequencies that mask ambient sounds and promote focus and relaxation.

Recommended ASMR & White Noise Content

1) Nature Sound ASMR

  • Rain sounds: Washes away mental clutter (10-hour versions recommended)
  • Ocean waves: The rhythmic pattern is excellent for meditation and sleep
  • Forest birdsong: Great for morning wake-ups or work focus sessions
  • Crackling fireplace: Cozy warmth for cold winter evenings

2) Everyday Sound ASMR

  • Page turning: Creates a reading-friendly atmosphere
  • Typing sounds: Boosts focus during work or study
  • Coffee brewing: Recreates the cafe ambience
  • Paper organizing: Simple, clean ASMR

3) Recommended YouTube Channels

  • Relaxing White Noise: High-quality white noise specialist
  • Calm: Curated sounds for meditation and sleep
  • Noisli: A platform where you can mix and match different sounds
Recommended Sounds by Situation
Work focus: Cafe ambience, white noise, typing sounds
Sleep: Rain sounds, ocean waves, deep white noise
Reading/studying: Library sounds, page-turning sounds
Meditation/relaxation: Forest sounds, meditation music, yoga BGM

#3: Slow TV & Observational Shows - Joy in the Everyday

Why Slow TV?

After an era dominated by sensational, fast-paced editing, "slow TV" and observational shows are now gaining popularity as healing content. Simply watching peaceful everyday life unfold - without competition or conflict - has a genuinely calming effect.

Recommended Slow TV Programs

1) Three Meals a Day (Samsi Sekki, tvN)

  • Format: Celebrities live in the countryside and cook meals from scratch
  • Healing factor: Slow-paced rural living and lovingly prepared home cooking
  • Recommended seasons: Mountain Village and Fishing Village editions

2) I Am Solo (SBS Plus/ENA)

  • Format: Genuine encounters and heartfelt conversations among single people
  • Healing factor: Natural, unforced relationship building
  • Highlights: Minimal editing that shows people as they truly are

3) Hyori's Homestay / Jinny's Kitchen (JTBC)

  • Format: Celebrity-run guesthouse reality shows
  • Healing factor: Beautiful scenery from Jeju Island and Mexico with a relaxed vibe
  • Highlights: Soothing music and an unhurried pace

4) Youn's Stay (tvN)

  • Format: Running a traditional Korean hanok (hanok) guesthouse
  • Healing factor: The beauty of Korean tradition in a refined setting
  • Highlights: Quiet, elegant storytelling
How to Get the Most Out of Slow TV
Watch on a weekend afternoon in comfy clothes on the couch
Try recreating a recipe featured on the show
Look up the show's background music playlist and listen
Watch with family or friends and chat along the way

#5: Meditation & Yoga Apps & Content - Your Digital Sanctuary

Why Meditation & Yoga Content?

Meditation and yoga are scientifically proven methods for stress relief. Thanks to modern apps and online content, they're easily accessible regardless of time or place, allowing you to find inner peace wherever you are.

Recommended Meditation & Yoga Apps

1) MABO (Mabo) - A Korean Meditation App

  • Highlights: Korean-language meditation guides with content tailored to Korean culture
  • Recommended programs: "Bedtime Meditation," "5-Minute Morning Commute Meditation"
  • Price: Free trial available, 9,900 KRW/month
  • Healing factor: A warm, familiar voice from Korean instructors

2) Calm - The #1 Global Meditation App

  • Highlights: Sleep stories, breathing exercises, meditation music, and more
  • Recommended feature: "Sleep Stories" (bedtime narratives)
  • Price: $69.99/year (7-day free trial)
  • Healing factor: Sleep stories narrated by celebrities

3) Headspace - Beginner-Friendly

  • Highlights: Step-by-step meditation courses with cute animated explanations
  • Recommended program: "Basics" series (for beginners)
  • Price: $69.99/year
  • Healing factor: A gentle, approachable guide that makes it easy to start

4) Cokkiri (Kokkiri) - A Korean Yoga App

  • Highlights: Yoga classes at various levels, plus Pilates
  • Recommended class: "Evening Yoga - Stretching for Better Sleep"
  • Price: 14,900 KRW/month (14-day free trial)
  • Healing factor: Professional yoga you can follow along with at home

Recommended YouTube Meditation & Yoga Channels

Free Alternatives - YouTube Channels

  • Yoga with Adriene: Yoga that beginners can easily follow along with
  • The Honest Guys: Guided meditation specialists
  • Healing Time Meditation: Korean-language guided meditation

Practical Tips for Healthy Media Consumption

Now that you've found some harmless content, it's important to learn how to consume it in a healthy way.

1. Set Digital Detox Times

  • First hour of the morning: Meditation or stretching instead of your smartphone
  • Mealtime: Focus entirely on your food with no content at all
  • One hour before bed: Block blue light; allow only nature documentaries or ASMR

2. Choose Content Intentionally

  • Plan ahead: "Today I'm going to watch a nature documentary"
  • Skip recommendations: Search for content yourself rather than relying on the algorithm
  • Build playlists: Create a playlist of only healing content

3. Manage Your Viewing Time

  • Set a timer: Use a 30-minute or 1-hour alarm
  • Turn off autoplay: Prevent unconscious binge-watching
  • Weekend special time: Keep it short on weekdays and enjoy more on weekends

4. Pair with Offline Activities

  • Try it yourself: Cook the recipes or do the yoga you saw on screen
  • Get outside: If you watched a nature documentary, take an actual walk in nature
  • Read: Swap some screen time for a physical book

5. Enjoy It Together

  • With family: Watch slow TV or nature documentaries together
  • With friends: Share and exchange good content recommendations
  • With pets: Listen to healing music or ASMR together
Warning Signs That You Need to Cut Back on Media
You feel even more tired after watching content
You watch out of habit even when you don't want to
You can't eat or sleep without content playing
It starts interfering with real life (work, relationships)
You frequently experience negative emotions (comparison, jealousy, sadness)

Lunchtime (10 minutes)

Evening (30 minutes)
